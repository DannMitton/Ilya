What is the same in every plate

  • The pill, the string, and the sage are the tree's own: IntakePanel.svelte:642-649, i18n.ts:171, --sage #8B9A7D at app.css:33.
  • The label is in the DOM twice, once stone and once white, and the white copy is clipped. A screen reader hears it once.
  • Every plate still assumes the loading pill escapes .action-btn:disabled's opacity: .45 (IntakePanel.svelte:1185-1188).
  • A warm cache sets progress straight to 1 (loader.ts:704, :712), so the 200 ms delay before the fill appears matters more for a vertical rise: a flash of a level jumping the height of the pill is more startling than a wipe.
