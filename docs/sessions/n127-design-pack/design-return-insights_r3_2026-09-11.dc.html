Insights, the third document: a squircle that sizes to its content, on paper that does not move

Six drawings: page one on the rose desk, the earned second page, the nearly empty page, the three clef passes over one compass, the consolidated foot in both languages, and the selector trio at the narrowest portrait width. Page one's budget is kept by curation, which demotes the two lighter findings to the earned page, so the frame never crosses prose. Every string on these sheets is an illustrative placeholder in the shape the engine's output would take, computed against the Sunless no. 1 fixture. No French is coined here; the French shown is i18n.ts:696 as the tree holds it, less the struck clause.

WHAT R2 CHANGED
The squircle sizes to its content. It opens under the head's rule and ends where the prose ends. The paper is what is fixed: 816 by 1056, 96 px sides, 48 px head and foot. Nothing is clipped and the frame crosses no line.
Curation keeps page one's budget. The two lighter findings by phonation mass, measures 5 to 8 and measure 27, are demoted whole to the earned page and leave a count behind. That is criterion 5 doing the work the frame was doing wrongly.
One apparatus block, one hairline. Footnotes, then the method line, then the attribution, all at 9.5 px on 1.45, above a single rose hairline. The page count sits alone beneath it. The attribution prints without its lieder.net clause.
Sized to the French. Every sheet reserves the height the French apparatus measures at this width, and English leaves the difference as space. Drawing 1f is the acceptance test.
Section headers take the metadata recipe. The sheet head's own .metadata-line, 14 px, 600, all small caps, 1.5 px tracking, in rose ink. One recipe now runs from the head into the squircle.
The compass leads, in a squircle of its own. The engraved stave is the first thing on the page, bound tight in the watch band's outline inside the page's, the graphic bleeding to that border with no interior margin, because it is what a singer researching repertoire reads first and it answers the choosing question without words. Ruling 8 opens page one with the at-a-glance fit; this treats the stave as that glance and puts the table's numbers under it.
The clef follows the singer. It is not fixed: clef-select.ts chooses among treble, treble-8vb and bass on the median against middle C, and drawing 1g sets the same compass in all three. Page one's sheets draw the bass pass because the fixture is a bass.
The stave carries its clef, in Maestro. The F clef is Finale Maestro's own glyph, SMuFL U+E062 at 56 px, four staff spaces to the em on this 14 px stave, baseline on the F line, the same face and the same wiring the Loupe and the correction surface already use. It opens the stave, so the lines are G2, B2, D3, F3, A3 by notation and not by caption, and the passaggi now rule where a low male voice turns: primo A3 on the top line, secondo D4 above it. The pitches are engine values, so correct them if the calibration says otherwise.
The labelled blank is gone. No rules, no heading, no invitation. What remains is the space between the squircle's last line and the foot, unlabelled, belonging to whoever holds the pen.

What the drawing shows, and the one thing it exposes
Ledger cost is why the selector exists: this piece costs two lines in bass, two in treble-8vb, and five in treble. The rose band behind each stave is that singer's declared range, so the compass is read against the voice and not against the page.
The exposure: chooseClef can only return treble or bass from the median. The treble-8vb pass is reachable today through clefFromSource alone, when the score's own clef carries the octave displacement, so a tenor whose declared median sits below middle C takes bass on a source that prints no clef. The tenor panel above is drawn as the pass intends, not as the median would currently route it. Whether the median should reach that pass is yours.

Measure 12, the E♭ at the top of the phrase, the vowel [u] in глубокая
Your highest note in this piece arrives on your quietest vowel. Cross the passaggio on the syllable before it, so the [u] begins already voiced and the top note continues a sound rather than starting one. Practise the two syllables as one gesture, then restore the consonant.1
3 further instances. The score carries each one, squircled in place.
The findings page one deferred, with their advice
Measures 5 to 8, the descent on тесная
Nine crossings of your primo passaggio inside four measures, six of them on unstressed syllables.
Nine crossings in four measures is a phrase that tires before it is learned. Sing the whole phrase on one vowel until the register change stops announcing itself, then put the text back a syllable at a time from the end.2
The score carries all nine crossings by measure.

HOW TO READ THIS DRAWING
Both feet are set at the sheet's real geometry, 624 px between the margins, so the two blocks below can be compared line for line. The two dashed rules stand where the French footnote and method lines will go. Those strings are not coined, so Design will not write them; the rules hold their space at the same 9.5 px on 1.45 the rest of the block uses, which is what the reserve must cover. The French attribution beneath them is the tree's own string with the lieder.net clause struck, and it is the longest line in either language.
Measured, at the sheet's own 624 px measure and the block's 9.5 px on 1.45: the English attribution sets in 2 lines, 28 px, and the French of i18n.ts:696 sets in 2 lines, 28 px. They are the same height. Page one's whole apparatus is 69 px inside a 103 px foot, so the French costs nothing over the English and the reserve holds with 34 px spare.
The condition still binds on the two owed strings: if the French footnote and method lines come back longer than their English counterparts by more than one line, that 34 px goes, the reserve grows, and the squircle ends higher. Nothing else moves.
