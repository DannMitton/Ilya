desk goes full width, the sheet fits itself to the width, the portrait reading
aid and its "Read" button appear under the page, and the one-station-at-a-time
rule applies. **A 1366 laptop is a phone as far as this app is concerned.**

**TWO SITES DID NOT COME WITH IT, AND THEY ARE N.70's.** The score uploader's

**WHY THE STORED SHAPE CHANGED, AND THIS IS THE ONE PLACE I DEPARTED FROM THE

array". Writing a bare `[]` cannot make "once" true. After increment 1 your own

writer it has always been and only its bytes changed.

station.
