**Before you begin.** Please tell me a little about yourself in this: teacher, coach, or singer (or all three), your voice type, and whether you used a phone or a computer. Set aside about an hour, with one song you know well.

1. **Can you find your way without me?** Before you open the Guide, take your song from score to transcription. Where did you hesitate, hunt, or get stuck? I'd rather hear about the clumsy moments than the smooth ones.
2. **Did Ilya read your score faithfully?** Where do Ilya's notes or words differ from your copy, and how easily could you set them right?
3. **Is it a pleasure to use?** What, if anything, would make you want to come back to it, or keep you away?

**If you calibrated your own voice** (about ten minutes in the Voice section of the Drawer), please read INSIGHTS for your song:

4. Did any Insights comment on this song say something you would have said to a student singing it in that key? Which one, and why?
5. Did any comment say something you would not say, or that is not true of your voice on this song? Which one, and what is wrong with it?
6. After reading Insights, is there one thing you now know about singing this song that you did not know from the score alone?

**For everyone:**

7. **Is Ilya useful to you?** Tell me what you'd actually reach for it to do this month (in your studio, your coaching, your own practice), what you'd leave it out of, and whom you'd recommend it to, with what warning.
8. **What didn't I ask that I should have?**
