MUSSORGSKY | WITHOUT SUN, NO. 1: WITHIN FOUR WALLS MELODY

For your consideration

  • Bar 3: your [i] meets your first resonance here, so the tone will want to turn full and heady, toward a whoop. Consider relaxing the jaw and leaning the vowel toward [ɪ], giving it a touch more space, which can lift your first resonance clear of the pitch.
