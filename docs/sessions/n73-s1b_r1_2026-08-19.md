## 3. Where the tree beat this brief

### The shadow was switched off on the phone, and that is the actual cause

The brief attributes the flat-box reading on the phone to the shadow being too
weak against a light desk. The measured cause is that the phone had no shadow at
all. Three mobile blocks set `box-shadow: none` on the sheet:
`TitlePage.svelte` and `SubsequentPage.svelte` inside
`@media screen and (max-width: 767px)`, and `ReadingPaper.svelte` inside
`@media (max-width: 767px)`. Changing the base declaration alone would have
changed nothing Dann can see on his phone.

I deleted the three `box-shadow: none` declarations and left a comment at each
site saying why the shadow now stays. The `@media print` blocks keep theirs, so
the printed artefact is unchanged.

### Three rules in `+page.svelte` outranked the sheets

`.main-content.tab-transcription :global(.paper-page)` and its Learn and Guide
twins each set their own shadow. Those selectors carry two more classes than
`.paper-page` and `.reading-paper`, so a sheet's own declaration never reached
the screen. The brief's instruction to change the value in the components would
have been silently overridden.

I deleted all three and left a comment at `+page.svelte:2557` recording what
stood there. There is one ruled shadow now and each sheet declares it, because
the sheet is the same sheet on every desk.

### The brief names two sheets, and there are four

`VoiceProfilePane.svelte` carries its own `.paper-page`, twinned from
`TitlePage`, with the same `0 1px 6px` shadow and the same `box-shadow: none` in
two mobile blocks. It is the marked score's own sheet, which is the destination
§2 rules on, and definition of done 1 covers it. I gave it the same treatment.

### The paper does not run edge to edge on the phone

`.main-content` takes `padding: 0.5rem` at `+page.svelte:2833`, so the sheet
sits in an 8 px desk gutter. A 12 px blur lands inside it. The edge reads.

### `--sheet-width` had to move with the reading sheet

`+page.svelte` set `--sheet-width: 720px` for Learn and Guide, so the desk head
would sit flush with a 720 px sheet. With the reading sheet at 816 px that
override is wrong, and it is also redundant, because the block above it already
publishes 816. I deleted it and left the reason in its place at
`+page.svelte:2525`.

### One claim in the brief needs a name change

The brief says the phone's tab takes "11 percent of a 390 px screen". At 44 px
that is 11.3 percent, so the figure is right. What it does not say is that
`.drawer-body` reserves a matching 44 px gutter when the drawer is open, and
that gutter measures the touch target rather than the paint. I left it at 44 px
and rewrote its comment, because shrinking it to 20 px would let a control sit
under the extension.

### What I did not touch, and you should rule on

The transcription empty state reads "Tap the chevron at the bottom to open the
drawer." The pull moved to the side in S1 and there is no chevron at the bottom
any more. It is a copy fix in two languages, it is outside this brief's four
items, and it is visible on the phone on first load.

## 4. My own walk

Measured on the live render at `localhost:5173` with `getBoundingClientRect` and
`document.elementFromPoint`, not read off the stylesheet.

**The sheet on the phone.** 375 by 812, coarse pointer, portrait. All four
destinations report `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35) 0px 3px 12px 0px` on the sheet. The
shadow is visible against the 8 px gutter on the bottom edge and both sides.

**The four surfaces.** Transcription: bar `rgb(139, 154, 125)`, desk
`rgb(209, 215, 203)`. Marked score: bar `rgb(142, 126, 155)` `#8E7E9B`, desk
`rgb(210, 203, 215)` `#D2CBD7`. Learn: bar `rgb(166, 123, 123)`, desk
`rgb(219, 202, 202)`. Guide: bar `rgb(92, 115, 158)`, desk
`rgb(190, 199, 216)`. Only the marked score changed.

**The pull, and how I confirmed the target.** I hit-tested it rather than
measuring the box. Walking `document.elementFromPoint` one pixel at a time along
the tab's centre line, and then down its centre column, gives the region that
actually resolves to the button:

| state | visible tab | hit-tested target |
|---|---|---|
| closed, x from the left edge | 0 to 20, height 76 | 0 to 43, height 88 |
| open, x from the left edge | 355 to 375, height 76 | 331 to 375, height 88 |

A point at x 36, which is 16 px past the paint, resolves to `.drawer-lip`.
Clicking that resolved point opened the drawer on the first press. Open, the
whole 44 px sits on-screen inside the reserved gutter.

**The margins.** 1280 wide, drawer closed. Every destination puts its sheet at
232 to 1048, 816 px wide, with 232 px of desk on each side, and the desk head
flush with the sheet's left edge on all four. At 1024 wide the four agree again
at 104 px each side. Learn and Guide land a 624 px prose column, which is the
transcription's text column to the pixel.

**One limit worth knowing.** The two sheets still behave differently when the
desk is narrower than 816 px, which happens on a 1024 px display with the drawer
open. The transcription sheet is a fixed 816 and overflows the desk; the reading
sheet is fluid and shrinks to fit. That predates this item and I did not change
it. What moved is the desk width at which the reading sheet stops matching,
from 784 px to 880 px.
