## 1. The two places the brief overrides the prototype

1. **One map at every size.** The prototype puts Metadata on the Piece band
   only at 1366 × 768 and as a row elsewhere. Build the band affordance at
   every size, phone included. Desk ruling: two desktops must not show two
   maps.
2. **Back sits on the LEFT of the takeover's band**, where `Drawer.svelte:619`
   puts it today. The prototype has it on the right; reverse the flex order.

## 2. What is ruled, in one place

- Three groups, top to bottom: **Piece** (Repertoire, the intake, Export and
  import; Metadata as an affordance on the band), **Text** (Notation,
  Analysis), **Score markup** (Underlay, Corrections, Voice). Ten stations
  become nine; the intake has no station row, it is always open.
- Groups are frames: no chevron, never closed, all station names visible at
  the opening state, which fits without scrolling at 1400 × 900, 1366 × 768,
  and 430 × 932 (measured by Design: 702, 661, 734 px).
- A station opens in place. **The drawer never scrolls itself**, except once,
  on entry to the calibration takeover. Nothing else moves without the
  singer.
- Desktop: any number of stations open. Phone (the existing 767 px rule):
  one at a time; opening a second closes the first; groups and headings do
  not move.
- The slab (`.drawer-body`) takes the desk's surround (`--surround-transcription`
  under the Transcription document, `--surround-marked` under Score markup);
  the groups carry `--drawer-bg`. The bookmark tab belongs to the slab, 20 ×
  152, centred, as ruled 2026-08-18.
- **20 px** is the fourth radius, a surface radius, for the three groups and
  the takeover frame. The ruled set is now 0, 4, 20, full-round.
- **Bands**: full colour, white text, the language-chip tokens:
  Piece `--lang-chip-guide` (= `--quiet-cobalt #5C739E`, 4.77:1), Text
  `--lang-chip-transcription #6C7A5F` (4.58:1), Score markup
  `--lang-chip-marked #806E8E` (4.63:1). Label recipe from
  `StationHeader.svelte`. Do not add a hex.
- **The Transcribe station is dissolved.** The Transcribe action lives under
  the intake in Piece and nowhere else. The word count and Clear live on the
  intake's receipt line.
- **Calibration stays a takeover**, entered from Calibrate in the Voice
  station, restore-on-exit as today, restyled: the Score markup band across
  the top with Back on its left, the 20 px surface, the station label
  recipe.
- The phone pull is the labelled pull ruled 2026-08-18.
- **English only.** French is deferred by Dann's ruling; every new string
  ships with its French key absent and the English shown in both languages
  until the strings table is ruled. Say which keys are new in each memo.
- Motion: one duration, `--motion: 180ms ease-out`, opacity and transform
  only; height is not animated.

The structure and the look, with today's stations moved into the groups and
today's two intakes left as they are inside Piece (text area above, score
drop below). No new behaviour except the grouping, the bands, the slab, the
tab, the phone rule, and the migration.

  `VoiceAnchor.svelte`, `sections.svelte.ts`: read them whole first.
- Migration of `ilya:openStations` exactly as Design's r2 memo §4.4 wrote it
  (the memo is in the tree): map `piece` to `metadata`, `songs` to
  `repertoire`, `analysis` to `analysis`, `shiftLyrics` to `underlay`; drop
  `source` and anything unrecognized; on a phone keep only the first
  survivor; write back under the same key so it runs once;
  `FIRST_RUN_STATIONS` becomes the empty array; `notation` stays
  unpersisted. New wire ids, never renamed after: `repertoire`, `metadata`,
  `binder`, `notation`, `analysis`, `underlay`, `corrections`, `voice`.

  station body under the band; at every size.

### Increment 2: the one intake

- One field in Piece that accepts paste, typing, a dropped file of any
  format `ScoreUploader.svelte` reads today, and the photograph reader.
  Text becomes the poem; a score file becomes the score; a photograph goes
  to the reader. The receipt line per kind, one line each, with count and
  Clear and Replace, as the prototype draws at `:365-:372`; a second file of
  a kind already present replaces that kind only.
- Sniffing: by file type and, for a dropped file with no useful type, by
  content (`<score-partwise`, MNX's JSON shape, the MuseScore zip, PDF's
  magic bytes, image types). A PDF asks once, in place, which it is
  (English placeholder string, key named). Do not guess.
- N.70 holds: no `accept` attribute on a coarse pointer.
- The Transcribe button under the field is the only Transcribe. The old
  Transcribe station and its `Clear` are removed; their strings stay in
  `i18n.ts` until the French table is ruled, marked unused.
- Done when: gates at baseline; on a local production build, paste a poem
  and the receipt says so and Transcribe draws the page; drop
  `~/Downloads/no-lyrics-control.musicxml` on the same field and the score
  receipt appears beside the poem receipt with the poem untouched; Replace
  on one kind leaves the other; Clear on one kind leaves the other; the
  photograph button reaches the reader; on the phone emulation the picker
  opens with no filter. Screenshots of the four states.
