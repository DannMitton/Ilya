## 1. What shipped

**The arrival view is the real page, scaled.** `Paper.svelte` wraps the page
stack in a scaler: the sheets keep their true 816 by 1056 layout and one
`transform: scale(k)` with `transform-origin: top left` fits them to the desk.
`k` is the desk's measured content width times 0.74, over 816. The stack is
taken out of flow and `.paper-fit` reserves `naturalHeight × k`, so nothing
below it collapses and the desk gains no scroll width. There is no second
drawing of the page anywhere: what the singer reads on the phone is the same
component tree the desktop lays out and the printer prints.

**One labelled action under the page.** `Read`, at `+page.svelte:2120`, in the
flow under the sheet. It appears only when the document has lines.

**The aid.** `apps/web/src/lib/components/ReadingAid.svelte`, new. It carries
`The page`, the label `Reading aid, not the page`, the word stacks at full
size, a line rule at each poem break, and `· end of verse N ·` closing each
verse. It has no shadow, no page edges, no header block, and no colophon, and
it is full bleed, which is the fastest way to tell it from a sheet without
reading a word. It renders through the same `VerseLine` and `WordStack` the
page renders, so the IPA still comes verbatim from the GraysonEngine and the
clitic and reconstitution rules are not written twice.

**Both views stay mounted.** The one not showing goes off-stage at
`left: -100000px`, not to `display: none`. `TitleHeader` measures itself with
`bind:offsetHeight` and a `display: none` element measures 0, so hiding the
page that way would collapse the row budget to its 9-row fallback and
re-paginate the document on every tap. Each view keeps its own scroll
position (`+page.svelte:324`).

**The interstitial is dead.** The gate, its three `mobile.*` strings, its
`mobileDismissed` state, its dismiss handler, and its eleven CSS rules are
gone. Nothing replaced it.

**The false copy is fixed.** `paper.empty.mobile` now reads "Tap the chevron on
the left to open the drawer." and « Appuyez sur le chevron à gauche pour ouvrir
le tiroir. », exactly as ratified.

I wrote no French that is not in the ratified table. The mockup's caption "The
page · rotate for full size" is not built, in either language.

I did not run `git`.

## 3. NOT ESTABLISHED, and what I did about each

### Where a poem breaks: NOT ESTABLISHED in the tree, recoverable from the input

`processText` drops blank lines at its first step
(`pipeline.ts`, `text.split('\n').filter((line) => line.trim())`) and numbers
what survives. `LineData` carried `lineNumber` and `words` and nothing else.
Stanzas, verses, and poem breaks did not exist anywhere in the transcription
path. The score-parser's `verse` is a different thing: it is a lyric verse
under one melody, not a poem's stanza.

So the aid's two ruled marks, the line rule and `· end of verse 1 ·`, had no
data source. **I did not invent one.** I carried the singer's own blank lines
across instead: `LineData.endsStanza` is set once, in `processText`, from
whether the next raw line is blank, and from nothing else.

**Tagged JUDGEMENT, and it is the one interpretation in this item:** a poem
breaks where the singer left a blank line. Nothing ruled that, and I can
defend it only as the reading a singer's own typing gives. If you want the
break to mean something else, this is the line to change, and the revert is
the `endsStanza` field plus `reading-aid.ts`.

The page does not draw it. Only the aid does, and the aid does not print.

### The aid's drawer pull: not built, by your brief's instruction

Ruling 5 lists "a labelled drawer pull" among the aid's parts, and the ratified
table carries `Drawer` / `Tiroir` for it. §3 and §6 of the brief say the
drawer's side tab is unchanged from S1b and I do not touch it. I followed the
brief. The S1b pull works on both views. **The labelled pull from ruling 5 is
unbuilt and unnumbered.**

### The interstitial's residue: nothing built, by §4

You have never ruled where a residue goes, so there is none. No toast, no
one-time note, no line in the drawer.

### Tapping a word in the aid: not wired

The page opens the inspector on a word tap. The aid does not: nothing ruled it,
and `onwordclick` is simply not passed. Say the word and it is one prop.

## 4. Where the tree beat this brief

The brief names the scaling and the aid. It does not name the four rules that
would have made a scaled page look wrong, and they were the larger half of
the work. Each is an N.45 spike block, and portrait C is the ruling that
retires N.45's portrait accommodation.

1. **`TitleHeader.svelte` hid the header block on the phone**
   (`visibility: hidden` below 767px), on the reasoning that a phone showed a
   content view and the metadata was duplication. Definition of done 1 names
   the header block as part of the page's dress. Deleted.

2. **`RunningHeader.svelte` hid the running head** for the same reason. A page
   that is visible needs the head that names it. Deleted.

3. **`PageFooter.svelte` rebuilt the whole footer below 767px**: the footer
   went `position: static` and flowed after the content, which is why the
   colophon rendered at the TOP of the fitted page before I found it; the page
   number was hidden because pages were not visible; and the attribution
   consolidated onto the last page alone. All three contradict "what is on the
   small page is what prints". The media query and its two comment blocks are
   deleted, and with them the N.45 residual they recorded.

4. **`TitlePage.svelte` and `SubsequentPage.svelte` reflowed the sheet**:
   `width: 100% !important`, `height: auto !important`, and the content window
   taken out of its absolute frame. That is the short card at no particular
   proportion, and it is the thing portrait C exists to cure. Both blocks are
   deleted, along with the `isMobile` override on the header measurement,
   which existed only because the reflowed title wrapped.

Three more consequences the brief does not name:

5. **`Paper.svelte` gave the phone the whole document's legend on the last page
   only** (`isMobile ? documentLegend : perPage`). Under WYSIWYG that is the
   wrong legend on every page, so the mobile branch is gone and the phone gets
   the per-page legend the printer gets.

6. **`Paper.svelte` closed the seam between pages on the phone** (`gap: 0`),
   which was right when the pages were one scroll and wrong now that they are
   discrete sheets. The desktop's 2rem is back and it scales with everything
   else.

7. **Print had to be defended explicitly.** `isMobile` is a width test, so a
   phone printing in portrait still carries the fit. Without a guard the
   printer would be handed a page scaled to a third of its size inside a box a
   third as tall. `Paper.svelte`'s `@media print` block undoes every part of
   the fit with `!important`, which beats the inline style, and `+page.svelte`
   hides the action and the aid and brings the page back on stage whichever
   view is showing.

One smaller thing: the interstitial's dismiss handler also called
`loadDictionary` a second time and then scrolled the desk by hand to centre an
816px page in a 390px window. The first is redundant (`onMount` already loads
it) and the second is obsolete (the page now fits). Both went with the gate.
