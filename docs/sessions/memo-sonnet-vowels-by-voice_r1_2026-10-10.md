## Question 3. How does each author explain a vowel's suitability?

### Coffin 1964: cavity frequencies, with tradition

- "Vowels are caused by two cavities, the cavity of the mouth, and the cavity of the pharynx." (p. 19; PDF p. 2).
- "The frequencies of these cavities for the various vowel,s are the same for men and women." (p. 19; PDF p. 2).
- On the harmonic reading: "At the same time the fundamental of the pitch being sung will be heard. If it is not, the tone will be a fraction of what it should be." (p. 19; PDF p. 2).
- On what modification is for: "to avoid a forced, uniform phonetic forming of vowels on all pitches of all voices. Such inflexible treatment might impair the musicality, expressiveness and survival of many voices." (p. 10; PDF p. 1).

### Titze 2007, 2009, 2012: source-filter interaction (inertance and reactance)

- "Hence, it is better to keep the harmonics away from the formants." (p. 199; PDF p. 1).
- "nonlinear source-filter theory predicts a preference in placing harmonics on the left side of a formant (below the formant frequency) as opposed to the right side (above the formant frequency)." (p. 199; PDF p. 1).
- "Stable and acoustically strong productions result when each of these harmonics (F0, 2F0, 3F0, and 4F0, as labeled on top) resides in high inertance territory, which is to the left of a formant." (p. 200; PDF p. 2).
- "Thus, the singer modifies the vowels to maintain consistency of harmonic energy throughout the pitch range." (pp. 200-201; PDF pp. 2-3).
- On register: "The new theory also makes a strong connection between vowel modification and voice registers (sometimes called lifts)" (p. 201; PDF p. 3).
- On what "favourable" means: "For readers familiar with descriptions of pitch-vowel interaction in terms of formant frequencies, the message is that 'favorable' translates to 'harmonics being below a formant' in frequency." (p. 330; PDF p. 2, Titze 2009).
- On the passaggi: "Thus, it appears that the primo passaggio in females is all about 2F0 interaction with F1, and the secondo passaggio is all about F0 interaction with F1." (p. 331; PDF p. 3, Titze 2009).
