## Part B. N.95: the barline detector finds zero barlines on real scans

From memo N.95: `reader.py:879` accepts barline candidates at `w <= 6` px
absolute, while true barlines on the 400 dpi scan measure 8 to 10 px at
`s = 30`. Zero barlines were found on the whole page, which silently breaks
the reader's measure, onset, and accidental-carry arithmetic.

1. Replace the absolute width ceiling with a scale-relative one. Derive the
   ceiling from measurement, not from taste: measure true barline widths on
   `tools/e16-harness/scans/raster400-1.png` AND on the render corpus the 23
   fixtures come from, then choose the bound as a fraction of `s` that admits
   both populations. State the derivation in a comment at the site.
2. Add the span test, ruled by Dann 2026-08-24: a barline extends from the
   top exterior staff line to the bottom exterior staff line and no further,
   within a stated tolerance. A candidate that stops short or overshoots is a
   stem or other ink, not a barline. This is the graphic discriminator
   between barline and stem; width alone is not.
3. Optional, only if cheap: a harness-side diagnostic (not app code, not a
   detection input) that sums read durations between detected barlines
   against the time signature and prints mismatches per measure.

Expectation to state before the verification run: scan page 1 carries 9
measures across 3 systems (memo N.95 correspondence section), so the detector
should report barlines consistent with 3 measures per system; zero is the
failing baseline.
