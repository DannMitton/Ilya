## Ruled by Dann

6. **The app bar keeps and re-keys.** Same bar, sigil, and language toggle;
   colour keyed to the three destinations: Studio sage, Learn rose, Guide
   cobalt.
7. **The drawer pulls.** Mobile: one labelled pull (the ⌃ DRAWER bar) replaces
   both unlabelled handles, on the fitted-page arrival and on the reading aid.
   Desktop: the lip becomes the **bookmark tab** (option A of
   `docs/sessions/ilya-lip-options_r1_2026-08-18.html`): a flat tab flush with
   the drawer's edge, drawer-paper fill `#FAF8F5`, hairline border, rounded on
   its outward corners only, ~76 px tall, chevron flips with drawer state.
8. **The Underlay station.** A fifth named station holding the pairing work
   (queue and cursor, slide operations, Start over), between Source and
   Analysis in the scroll, with an honest empty state when no score is loaded.
   English name: **Underlay**. French: NOT RULED; candidates shown to Dann
   (« répartition » recommended, « paroles », « pose du texte »); ships only
   with the full French strings table.
9. **The contextual-sentences design for the slide operations.** Diagnosis
   accepted: Ilya transplanted Finale's verbs without their sentence structure
   (object first, then verb; hidden until wanted). The cure, ruled:
   - Click grammar unchanged (N.55b R4): click an undecided note, the cursor's
     syllable lands.
   - New: clicking a note that already carries a pairing **selects** it. The
     Underlay station then shows that pairing, named, with its repairs as plain
     sentences: "Slide this syllable and everything after it, one note later,"
     and "one note earlier."
   - The verbs render only when a paired note is selected. Before any
     selection, the station shows only the queue and the cursor.
   - Finale's two scopes collapse to one automatic rule: a slide runs until
     the first open note absorbs it, or to the end if none does. No scope
     vocabulary in the UI.
   - Rotate appears only when a multi-note selection exists.
   - The insert-with-ripple alternative was costed and **rejected**: it would
     rewrite R4's ruled click semantics.
   - Nothing in N.55b's pairing map, states, or storage changes. Presentation
     and scope arithmetic only.
