## 2. Ruled design

- One button in the Loupe's Syllables tab, in the pill row under the syllables beside Clear placements and Place from here.
- Pressed on a selected note whose syllable is not the last of its unit, it resolves to the unit's last syllable, writes «\u00A0#» into the Lyrics text after that unit (Dann 23:52), and the Loupe's selection (the squircle) jumps to the note carrying that syllable.
- A unit is a word with its proclitics (forward), its enclitics (back), and a particle the poem joined by a hyphen («велит-ли»). A polysyllabic word is one word.
- The `#` is visible inline in Lyrics and on the IPA line of Text and Markup, as a bare `#` (Dann 23:38), never in Poem, never in the Cyrillic, never on a staff. It exists only where the singer placed it, or where the score placed it (Dann 23:52 (a): a score's own `#` reads as placed and can be removed).

DESK DEFAULT, his to wave off: the button is off where the unit already ends in a hard boundary the engine took for itself. A spaced dash is not such a boundary (Dann 23:53), so the button is on before one.

### What `UndoHistory` does, and what that fixes in the build

Read to its end on 2026-10-21; the line numbers below were re-read on 2026-10-25 and corrected where the review found them off (amendment 10).

- `UndoState` is `apps/web/src/lib/score/undo-history.svelte.ts:44-71`. `push(note)` snapshots the state as it stands when called and clears the redo stack (`:111-114`), so the press must push BEFORE it writes the text.
- `undo` pushes a snapshot of the state now onto the redo stack, writes the taken entry, and pops it (`:116-122`); `redo` mirrors it (`:124-130`). Both snapshot through `#snapshot`, which calls the page's `read` with no knowledge of the verb (`:104-106`).
- `clear` empties both stacks and is called on a song switch (`:138-141`), so a break entry cannot cross songs. `stackLabel` composes the pill's sentence (`:148-152`).
- The page builds it at `+page.svelte:847-864`, with the destructure at `:865`; step 0 moves that block to `undo-wiring.svelte.ts`.
- Nineteen push sites exist today: thirteen in `apps/web/src/lib/score/correction-verbs.svelte.ts` (`:201`, `:210`, `:239`, `:257`, `:265`, `:274`, `:288`, `:296`, `:331`, `:354`, `:376`, `:381`, `:389`), one in `apps/web/src/lib/score/tuplet-row.svelte.ts:78`, and five in `+page.svelte` (`:636`, `:667`, `:702`, `:1243`, `:1273`). `apps/web/src/lib/score/undo-history.test.ts:43` builds an `UndoState` literal. (r4 counted sixteen; amendment 10. The conclusion below holds for nineteen.)
- **The flaw in the first r4, found by the read:** adding `inputText` as a required field makes EVERY verb's undo restore `inputText` to what it was at that push. A singer who types a word in Lyrics (which pushes nothing), then presses Undo on an earlier placement, would lose the typing. Today an undo leaves `inputText` alone. That change is out of this brief's scope and walls a singer in.
- **The build:** `inputText` is optional on `UndoState`. `push` takes an option that adds the current `inputText` to the snapshot; `undo` and `redo` add the current `inputText` to the opposite stack's snapshot only when the entry being taken carries one; the page's `write` assigns `doc.inputText` only when the entry carries one, through `handleInput(entry.inputText, 'paste')`, AFTER `doc.pairings` and `doc.seatedText` are restored (`:855-861`), so the transcription that follows diffs the restored seated text against the restored input (`:1971`) and reseats nothing. Test: undo after the press leaves `lines` equal to the pre-press `lines` and `doc.pairings` equal to the pre-press map. (Amendment 1, the review's text.) The nineteen existing sites and the test literal stay valid, and none of them touches `inputText`.
- **What the singer gets:** Undo after the press removes the `#` (with its `\u00A0`). Text typed after the press is lost by that undo and comes back with Redo (redo's snapshot is taken at undo time, `:119`). A new action clears Redo (`:113`), so typing after an undo and then acting loses it for good; say so in your report and do not hide it.
- **Tests, each failing before the change:** press then undo restores the text byte for byte, and `lines` and `doc.pairings` equal their pre-press values; undo then redo returns the text with «\u00A0#» and its IPA; an Undo of a different verb after typing leaves `inputText` as typed; `seatedText` and `inputText` are equal after the break's undo when they were equal at the push (the entry saves both at the same moment); a song switch empties the stacks.
