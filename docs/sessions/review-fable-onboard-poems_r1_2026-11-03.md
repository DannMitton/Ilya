## Reasons

### 1. Fill or offer

The two principles in tension are both Dann's: process what arrives with no button (`PRODUCT.md`, "Once there is data to process", 2026-09-16) and never surprise the singer about which words end up under the notes (`PRODUCT.md`, "The two sets of words", N.121). A library fill does not touch the words under the notes by itself: Lyrics is the master for the words (default 2), and the only writers a poem reaches are tier 1 (quiet, dotted underline, Revert on the receipt line, items 6 and 8) and the ink-only half of item 1 as amended. So a fill is a change to Poem, a view the singer did not type in, plus a bounded set of marked repairs. That is the shape of a default with an exit, not a surprise. The exit must exist on the line itself (change 7), because the replace guard (`intake.poemReplace`) is for the singer's own poem and a library poem is not theirs.

### 2. The public-domain test

The draft's Canada rule matches what the desk checked on 2026-10-02 (N.178 item 17: non-retroactive extension, died 1971 or earlier). Its "stricter worldwide" rule is not worldwide: the United States counts from publication, not death, and Russia extends terms for rehabilitated and war-service authors. A rule stated as "died at least 70 years ago" also drifts by a year, since the term runs to the end of the calendar year. The fix is to record the facts per entry and compute the status at build time (change 3), so the shipped set is a flag and not a re-curation. "Never translations" would remove Marshak's sonnets, which are Canada-clear since 2015 and are the reader's build material; the test belongs on the Russian text's author.

### 3. A wrong poem matched with confidence

Where it goes wrong: shared titles and incipits («Ночь», «Молитва», «Весна»); several Russian translations of one Heine or Goethe poem sharing words and metre; cycles with refrains; a page read as almost nothing (T5: song 1 p2 s1 returned dashes only; song 2 p1 s1 returned «І эоъ дь пи ъѵ ът п»). What a wrong fill can do: a wrong source line; sage and grey over most of both views; tier 1 repairs. Tier 1 needs the scan's word to fail the dictionary, the poem's aligned word to pass, a known confusion or a character-set garble, a confident alignment at the spot, and the syllable count unchanged (item 6). Item 9 makes exact long-word matches the confidence signal. So if the match score and the alignment's anchors are the same instrument (change 6), a wrong poem yields no anchored stretch, and tier 1 cannot fire inside one; what is left is colour, which the dismiss clears. The bound is measured, not argued: the held-out set in change 6 is where it is tested, and the margin is between the top candidate and the second, not an absolute score.

### 4. The loop and the gate

The loop as drafted trains on the build songs with library poems as labels. The build songs already have truth: the six *Sunless* songs and the ten sonnets from Dann's engravings, the Tchaikovsky by eye (N.178 items 15 and 16). A poem aligned to a scan is a weaker label than a truth file at every row where the composer repeated, omitted, or altered (song 1 sets stanzas 1 and 3, with variants, T6 report), so on the 18 the poem replaces a better label with a worse one. Its value is on songs with no truth, which is where R5 (fine-tuning on real rows) was parked for want of data. The gate: row 114's texts can enter the model by a route the draft does not name, a wordlist or DAWG built from the library (R2 tried a syllable DAWG). Change 9 closes it. The confidence threshold has the same leak if it is tuned where it is measured; the draft already says the build songs set it, and change 6 adds that row 114 never does.

### 5. What the draft misses

- **The loader's hash.** `loadDictionary` keys every chunk on `manifest.hash` (`loader.ts:716`, `:741-743`), the gloss tier on the same hash (`:546`), and `cleanLegacyCache` deletes everything under `dict-` and `gloss-` that is not the current hash (`:314-320`). Item 7's default for the table of spellings carries the same gap; change 5 fixes both.
- **The worker's cache.** `sw.js:100` exempts only `dictionary.*.json` from interception; everything else is stale-while-revalidate (`:129-142`) and is deleted on activate (`:58-68`). A library in that cache lags one open after a deploy and vanishes on the next.
- **Stored songs.** Default 3's last sentence (existing songs: Poem starts empty, no seating moves) and the rule over all of it (a poem change re-tests every hand word) together mean a fill on open would spray tier 3 offers over a singer's finished work. Change 6.
- **The Text pair.** N.186 draws the Poem | Lyrics pair in Text only when Poem differs from Lyrics; with a library, most songs will show it. Not a fault; the Guide paragraph owed under N.186 now describes the common case.
- **The trigger's inputs.** Step 2 uses "the title and composer if the reader found them"; no reader path reads a title (NOT ESTABLISHED in `ScoreUploader.svelte:185-235`, which routes by staves and OCR only). The Piece fields the singer may have typed are the signal that exists.
- **The poem-page route today.** `readAsPoemByOcr` creates its worker with `createWorker('rus')` and no `langPath` (`ScoreUploader.svelte:372-373`); where tesseract.js fetches `rus.traineddata` by default is NOT ESTABLISHED this session. If it is a CDN, the scan-a-poem-page route is neither offline nor on-device, and a shipped library is the better offline path to a Poem. Outside this draft; for `ENVIRONMENT.md`.
- **One file, several songs.** A cycle PDF fills Lyrics with several songs; the match is per song. Build 0 is one song per drop; the draft says nothing, and r2 says so.
- **Versions of the library.** The entry's version stamp is right; the document must also record which library version filled Poem, so a corrected text can be re-offered and §8's unratified rewording ("carries the engine's version") is met.

### 6. The smallest first build

Nothing in the scenario needs app code to be proven: whether the match finds the right poem from the scan's Lyrics, and what a real poem is worth on row 114, are both answerable in Node over the control captures, and T6 could not run because the poems were not obtained (`/root/desk/E/poems/` holds two 302 stubs). Build 0 is that measure with a real library of about 28 poems.
