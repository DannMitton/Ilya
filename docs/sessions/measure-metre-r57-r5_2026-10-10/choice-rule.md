# Row 57 r5: how the variant is chosen (written 2026-10-11 00:36 EDT, before any fold's earlier-seed totals were summed)

1. Each fold tunes each of the 12 variants on its 4 tuning songs and 300 tuning renders (0 false signatures there; then most right).
2. A variant is eligible if, summed over the 17 folds, it states 0 false signatures on the earlier render seeds (round 4's seeds 9000+f, the held-out music font).
3. Among eligible variants, the choice is the one with the most right signatures on the tuning songs, summed over the folds; ties go to the most found on the earlier seeds, then to fewer readings.
4. If no variant is eligible, the choice is the one with the fewest false signatures on the earlier seeds, ties as in 3.
5. The held-out songs' results are not looked at before the choice is written in choice.md.
