## 2. For each loss, where Gould says to look

The losses are from `report-code-the-baseline-on-eight-songs_r1_2026-10-02.md` and `report-code-length-is-read-from-shape_r1_2026-10-02.md`.

Built today as reach and travel; the faint ones abstain. A fitted flag shape is the trial's work |

## 3. Is it a synthesis we lack, or information?

**Mostly a synthesis.** The rules were in the project since July and August, sorted for drawing. Nothing had set them beside the reader's losses until this table.

**Two gaps are information, and only the book closes them:** rests (pp. 34 to 38) and notehead sizes with white noteheads (pp. 10 to 12). Both are on the shot list of 2026-09-14 as Tier 5. Rests are the brief after next.

**One witness was in the book and unused: spacing.** It needs no new information, only a measurement.
