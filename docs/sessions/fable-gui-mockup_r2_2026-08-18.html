Learn · Chapter 3 of 8

Stressed
Vowels

Five vowels do most of the singing. This chapter is where Russian starts to sound Russian.
Grayson §§4.1–4.912 min

Under stress, Russian vowels are at their most stable and most singable. A stressed vowel holds its full colour for the length of the note, which is why Grayson builds the entire system outward from the stressed syllable.

Consider любовь: the stress falls on the second syllable, and everything about the first is decided by that fact…

Guide

How Ilya
Works

What happens when you paste a poem, and why you can check every symbol it prints.
3 sections7 min

Ilya has twin purposes: to enable, and to educate. It equips singers with verifiable, accurate IPA transcriptions of sung Russian texts, following a method devised by Craig Grayson.

Ilya is rule-based and deterministic, not machine learning. When you see an IPA symbol in Ilya, it reflects Grayson's notation; when you see the same symbol in his dissertation, it means the same thing. That is the promise.

A Walkthrough ›
Guide opens the same way in cobalt. Same anatomy, different room: one band grammar serves both, so the singer learns it once.

Principles applied:

· The object owns its dress: header block, shadow, and colophon appear only on the page, never on the reading aid. The aid is labelled as an aid.

· Oversized type and full-strength colour are spent on arrival moments only: chapter bands and the portrait page greeting. The working drawer and the reading measure never inflate.

· Hue names place: rose is Learn's, cobalt is Guide's, at full strength on the band and nowhere else on the page.

· Recognition over recall: the drawer pull is labelled, the aid says what it is, the end of the verse says it ended.
