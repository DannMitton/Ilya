**On `:767`.** I nearly filed this under "correct as is", because *m.* looks the
same in both languages. It is not: your own loupe tag renders it « mes. » at
`:353`, `:356`, `:524`, and `:531`. **A singer in French mode meets « mes. » in
the loupe and "m." in the witness line**, which is the one-term-per-concept rule
broken inside one document.

**⚑ On `:94`, *Underlay*.** Your file has no French for it. Three candidates:
**« Placement des paroles »**, which names what the section governs and is my
recommendation; **« Paroles »**, plain and short, but it loses *under*;
**« Texte sous les notes »**, which matches `upload.banner.noLyrics:1222`
(« Votre texte se trouve sous les notes ») but is long for a heading.
