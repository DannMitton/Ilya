## Sources and method

- **Thesis**: Mitton, D. A. (2020), DMA thesis, University of Toronto. Appendix B, Sunless no. 2, printed p. 208 (PDF page 226). Intro to Appendix B, printed p. 206: grey stemless noteheads are "an octave below my fR1 values". Table 5.3 printed p. 81 (PDF p. 99); Figure 5.3 printed p. 82 (PDF p. 100).
- **Ilya rows (2018 values)**: `notes-mitton.csv`, the 68 rows with song = "Sunless 02", file lines 98 to 165 (row n below is file line 97 + n). Columns used: bar, beat, pitch, vowel, turning_pitch. The CSV has no syllable column, so the Cyrillic syllable comes from the thesis underlay (p. 208).
- **Ilya turning pitch, 2026**: computed here as fR1 / 2 for Ilya's vowel, using the 2026 list you supplied, as the nearest equal-tempered pitch (A4 = 440 Hz). Formula: MIDI = 69 + 12 log2(Hz/440), rounded.
- **Thesis turning pitch**: the pitch Figure 5.3 (p. 82) prints for the thesis vowel. I rendered p. 208 and p. 82 to images and read them, so this is a printed value, not only a derivation. I looked at the grey heads of the notes in bars 1, 2 (first three), 6, 7 (first nine), 8, 9 (last two), 10, 11 and 12 and each matched Figure 5.3. The remaining heads (bars 2 rest, 3, 4, 5, 9 first five) I did not inspect one by one; they follow from the vowel via Figure 5.3, which Appendix B says they share.
- **Alignment**: the thesis numbers its pickup as bar 1, as the CSV does. Each thesis bar holds the same number of sung notes as the CSV bar (1, 6, 9, 5, 6, 8, 9, 7, 7, 3, 6, 1 = 68), and the CSV's bar and beat values were paired with the thesis's syllables in reading order. Every one of the 68 rows aligned; none is flagged. The CSV pitch was not compared to the thesis staff note by note (the alignment rests on count and order per bar, plus the vowel agreement in 59 rows).
- **Cause**, as defined: `vowel` = the two vowels differ and Ilya's 2018 and 2026 heads are the same; `fR1` = same vowel and Ilya's 2026 head differs from its 2018 head; `both` = vowels differ and Ilya's head also moves with the 2026 values; `none` = same vowel and the head does not move. Where the heads coincide, the row is `none` or `vowel` by this rule.
- **Dagger (†)**: rows on [e]. Ilya's CSV head is G3 and the thesis prints F#3. Cause: 381 Hz halved is 190.5 Hz, which is 50.7 cents above F#3, a rounding tie (Table 5.3 itself gives F#4 +50 ct). The tie is not a vowel or a 2026 effect, so it is not counted in `cause`; it is only visible as G3 against F#3 in the two 2018 columns.

Counts by cause: vowel 1, fR1 35, both 8, none 24 (total 68).
