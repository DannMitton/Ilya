# Run sheet: the colleague walkthrough, r2

Serves N.83. Supersedes r1, which skipped calibration, the fit verdict, and
transposition. Length: roughly 25 minutes, an estimate, not a measurement.

**The structure changed, for a reason you cannot see on the sheet: Zoom
remote control moves the colleague's mouse, never their microphone.** A
calibration run over remote control would capture your room's audio, with
their voice arriving Zoom-compressed through your speakers. That reading
lies. So part 1 runs in the colleague's own browser, on their own
microphone, with you directing by voice. Part 2 is your shared screen,
where the scan files live.

## Before the call

- Send the colleague: `[deploy URL]` and the engraved Without Sun song 1
  file, so Fit has it locally in part 1.
- Have on your desktop: the 400 dpi PNG of the Lamm scan and the same
  engraved file.
- English interface throughout.

## Part 1. The colleague's browser, their voice

1. **Arrival.** They look, they do not click. Your line: "Ilya makes a study
   edition for singers of Russian song. The paper you see is the interface
   itself."
2. **Transcribe.** They paste a Russian poem. IPA appears on the page.
   Invite: "Click any word." The Inspector opens in the drawer, the
   correction redraws the page. Your line: "The page is the control. Nothing
   Ilya prints carries a widget."
3. **Calibrate.** They record their vowels. The roster fills honestly:
   captured values where the window held, Provisional with its reason named
   where it did not. Your line: "Ilya measures your voice. Where it is not
   sure, it says so instead of guessing."
4. **Fit.** They load Without Sun song 1. The watch band forecasts the fit
   for their voice, not a generic one. Your line: "Fit forecasts whether
   this piece suits this voice. It never declares."
5. **Transpose.** CONTINGENT: this beat exists only if N.94 ships and is
   walked before the call. They open the picker: an informed default,
   selected, and a free nudge either way. The paper redraws in the new key.
   Your line: "Ilya advises. The singer decides." Fallback if N.94 has not
   landed: show the watch band's transposition advice sentence and say the
   picker is in build.
6. **Learn.** They open two of the seven sections. Brief.
7. **Print.** Print preview of the transcription or score markup. Your line:
   "Print renders the paper. The page you studied is the page you hold."

## Part 2. Your shared screen

8. **The reader, framed honestly.** Upload the PNG. It reads in about six
   seconds and renders: 3 systems, 9 staves, 57 notes. Your line: "The
   mechanism runs end to end on a 1931 scan. The notation it renders is not
   yet accurate. That is the open work, and it is measured, not guessed."
9. **The contrast.** The same song, engraved, from part 1 beat 4. Your line:
   "From an engraved file, the same paper is accurate. The gap is scan
   recognition alone."
