## The walk, as the singer and as her coach

### Item 1, the printed page

- As the singer: she wants every page to end above its footer and no half-empty page in the middle. The brief says both. Good.
- As the engineer reading what she will get: the page breaks by an estimate (`rowsOf`, `page-config.ts:117-119`, seven words a row) against a budget of row slots (`sliceLinesToPages`, `:158-200`). Page 1's budget comes from `rowConfig` (`TitlePage.svelte:115-137`); every later page's budget is the constant `LINES_PER_PAGE` of 10 (`page-config.ts:97`, `:166`) with a fixed 28 px gap (`SubsequentPage.svelte:73`). The brief's wording "`page1Budget` measured once from the title page" is right but leaves the later pages out of the sentence; the fix must govern them too (amendment 3).
- A candidate cause for the 7.4 px, from the arithmetic (inference, marked as such, checkable in two minutes): `rowConfig` sizes ten rows and nine gaps into `availableHeight` (`TitlePage.svelte:118-123`) but the content window also carries `padding-top: 4px` (`:214`) that the arithmetic never subtracts, and the gap is rounded, not floored (`:136`). With the header at 127 px (`HEADER_HEIGHTS_AT_LETTER.title`, `page-config.ts:71`), available = 1056 − 191 − 136 = 729; gap = (729 − 560) / 9 = 18.78, rounded to 19; ten rows then occupy 560 + 171 + 4 = 735 px in a 729 px window: 6 px over. At a measured header of 128 or 129 px the same sums give 7 or 8 px over. The step 6 report's three states fit this: the poem-filled page holds exactly ten one-row lines (over by 6 to 8 px), and the unfilled page holds a cut head of a 95-word line that wraps to fewer rows than the estimate budgeted (56 px spare). The subsequent page has 11 px of clearance after its own padding (827 − 812 − 4), which is why only page 1 shows it. NOT ESTABLISHED until Code measures the header on that fixture.
- The footer is a second, separate way to overrun, and the brief does not mention it. Both Text pages reserve a constant `FOOTER_MAX_HEIGHT` of 80 px (`page-config.ts:84`; `TitlePage.svelte:101`; `SubsequentPage.svelte:66`). `PageFooter` offers `onheightchange` for exactly this reason ("so the page that owns it can reserve exactly that much and no content window ever reaches into it", `PageFooter.svelte:19-28`), and neither Text page passes it (`TitlePage.svelte:189-195`; `SubsequentPage.svelte:96-102`). The footer is anchored at the bottom and grows upward (`:107-108`), so a legend of several items in French lifts its top above the reserved 80 px and the test's footer edge with it (`pages-without-gaps.test.ts:68-72`). A fix that measures rows and keeps the footer constant can still overrun.
- Print: the brief asks Code to "state how print and screen are both covered". The code's own answer is that the sheet keeps 816 by 1056 on every display (`TitlePage.svelte:247-257`) and the print rules change colour, shadow, and the empty directive only (`TitlePage.svelte:259-268`; `SubsequentPage.svelte:141-146`; `WordStack.svelte:408-419`), so a measurement taken on screen at letter width holds in print, provided the same fonts are loaded. The brief can say that, and ask Code to confirm it rather than discover it.

## Amendments

2. **Part 1, the footer is a measured height too.** Wire `PageFooter`'s `onheightchange` on both Text pages and set `contentBottom` from it, with `FOOTER_MAX_HEIGHT` as the value before the first measurement. Reason: a legend taller than 80 px overruns however well the rows fit. Evidence: `PageFooter.svelte:19-28`, `:107-108`; `TitlePage.svelte:101`, `:189-195`; `SubsequentPage.svelte:66`, `:96-102`.

Say whether the gap on each page stays as computed or is recomputed from the measured sum (DESK DEFAULT: computed from the measured sum, with the same 18 to 28 floor and cap). Reason: `rowConfig` distributes rows of 56 px; a wrapped row is 56 + gap + 56 and the sum is wrong for it. Evidence: `page-config.ts:97`, `:166`; `SubsequentPage.svelte:73`; `TitlePage.svelte:115-137`; `VerseLine.svelte:87-93`.
4. **Part 1, one measurement, one pure slicer.** Measure in one place (each row's height, and each word's `offsetTop` within its line for the wrap points), and pass the result into `sliceLinesToPages` as data, so the slicer stays pure and unit-testable with synthetic heights; keep the two-rows-each-side cut rule on measured wraps (`page-config.ts:181`, `:188`).
