## 1. The change, by part

**Part A, the effective right initial.** `engine.ts:2181-2191` (was `:2179-2188`). `viaV` is true when the right word begins with в or вʲ. A voiceless left final before в is left exactly as before (`continue`; see the decision below). For a voiced left final, `initialPastV` (`voicing-rule2.ts`) skips the v inside the right word, or, for the bare proclitic «в», reads the first segment of the next word, not across a hard boundary. A vowel or a sonorant goes to the boundary #4 test. A voiced obstruent goes to the existing voiced-before-voiced branch, and a voiceless one to the existing devoicing branch. The sibilant mergers and the в-before-voiceless branch are unchanged, and they still read `rightInitial`, not the look-through result. The comment now quotes p. 251, not p. 250.

**Part B, how the engine learns the left word keeps its voicing.** `BoundaryWord.isProclitic?` (`engine.ts:116`). `keepsVoicingAtBoundary4` takes `word.isProclitic ?? proclitics.has(clean)`, or the contractions «б» and «ж», or membership in `VOICING_PREPOSITIONS`. Stress, reduction, `cliticData`, `proclitics`, `enclitics`, and `boundaries.ts` do not read it. No word was added. I think no word belongs that is not there, so there is nothing to list for Dann.
