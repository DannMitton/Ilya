## Notes on the choices

- **Row 1, "Compare" for "Review".** The accepted table already turned "Review the 5 flagged bars" into "Compare 5 bars with your score". The button takes the same verb. "The review" stays the desk's name for the feature in briefs.
- **Row 2, « Plus tard » over « Pas maintenant ».** « Pas maintenant » only declines. « Plus tard » says the offer comes back, which is true: any marked bar reopens it. It is also five letters shorter.
- **Row 10, « quand vous voudrez » over « quand vous serez prêt ».** « prêt » must agree with the singer (« prête »), and Ilya does not know which. « voudrez » needs no agreement.
- **Row 18.** "As Ilya read it" is kept here on purpose: Restore undoes the singer's own correction, so the thing returned to is Ilya's reading, not the print.
- **Row 21.** If the long forms do not fit the footer slot, the fallback is « Supprimer » alone, the tree's visible word today, with the thing named in the line over it.
