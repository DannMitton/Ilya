## 2. Could not establish

- **Singer-specific evidence.** I found no dedication or premiere by a named soprano or mezzo for any row. Treble association rests on recital programmes and recordings (E1 to E9), not on dedications.
- **IMSLP per-song voice labels.** Rachmaninoff Op.4, 8, and 14 list a voice per Muzgiz file (for example Op.4/4 is labelled "Tenor"). Sopranos and mezzos sing that song (E1, E7), so the labels look recording-based. I did not treat them as printed designations.
- **Dedicatees on Op.14.** The fetch tool paired them with songs by order, and the page does not map them. I did not use them.
- **Cyrillic titles.** The Op.4 page gives Cyrillic only for Nos. 1, 5, and 6, so Op.4/4 has none. The Medtner Op.13 page gives none either. Op.4/1 is listed on that page with a Cyrillic title.
- **Poets.** Not mapped per song on the page for Rimsky Op.3/4, Op.8/2, Rachmaninoff Op.26/12, and Op.4/1. The Arensky Op.27/1 poet comes from list order in the fetch summary.
- **Title matching.** Taneyev "Music" and Arensky "Song of the Little Fish" are matched to the recital titles by title and poet, not confirmed on the source.
- **Voice designations.** Only the files noted in the table state one. Rachmaninoff Op.21/5 "High voice" is an English/German Schirmer file. The Op.40 Belaieff 1900 file (#444902, "High voice", Rus/Fr) is a set listing, and the page does not say which songs it holds.
- **Glinka and Dargomyzhsky.** Their IMSLP pages are whole-collection pages with no per-song files. I read no voice designation for them.
- **Dargomyzhsky.** The only soprano lead is Briggs's "I'm Grieving" (Lermontov), which fits "Мне грустно" (Vol. I no. 36, #27395, Muzgiz 1947). The page gives no Cyrillic for it. The mezzo lead, "I loved you" (E7), I matched to "Я вас любил" (no. 13) by translation only. Neither is in the table.
- **Not pursued.** I did not pursue Lyadov or Glière. The Glière works page did not list Maxwell's titles. Alyabyev was fetched but is only one row. Rubinstein Op.8 lists no Russian-text file. Tchaikovsky Op.47/7 has a named soprano premiere but no Cyrillic title on its page (see I21).
- **E7 error.** The Lawrence-Hares programme assigns Glinka's "Do not sing, fair maiden" an "Op. 51 No. 2", which looks wrong. I ignored that item.
- **Excluded sets.** I fetched nothing on Tchaikovsky Op.38, Mussorgsky "Sunless", Kabalevsky, Gurilyov, Grechaninov, or Varlamov.
