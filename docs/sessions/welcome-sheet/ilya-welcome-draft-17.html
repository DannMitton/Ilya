Sung Russian made easier.

Ilya can make mistakes. Ilya bundles a live IPA transcription engine, an onboard Russian dictionary with English and French glosses, optical music recognition (OMR) that reads scores by machine, and an acoustic module that estimates your resonances using the microphone in your own device. Any of these steps can go wrong. So treat what Ilya shares as an early consultation within a much longer process of studying your song or aria, and confirm anything Ilya tells you with your teacher or coach before you rely on it.

The method is Craig Grayson’s: Russian Lyric Diction (University of Washington, 2012).

Made by Dann Mitton, voice teacher and researcher, dannmitton.com. His doctoral research, Sung Russian for the Low Male Voice Classical Singer (University of Toronto, 2020), is the argument behind Ilya; ilya.dannmitton.com is its home.
