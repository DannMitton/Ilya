**HOW "THE FRENCH KEY ABSENT" IS BUILT, AND IT IS NOT LITERALLY ABSENT.** The
brief asks for the French key absent and the English shown in both languages.
Under today's `t()` those two cannot both be true: `t()` returns
`[MISSING: key]` for an absent language variant (`i18n.ts`), so a truly absent
French key would put that literal on the band of a French session's drawer.
**I put the English in the `fr` slot** and marked the block, which is the
tree's own precedent for a value that is the same in both slots (the `tab.*`
block). What I did not do is build a fallback mechanism, because that is new
machinery for a table you are about to rule. If you would rather see
`[MISSING: group.piece]` on the band until the table is ruled, that is one
line each.

**`group.scoreMarkup` duplicates `tab.markedScore`'s English**, which already
carries ratified French, « Partition annotée » (2026-08-19). I kept them apart
because one names a document in the desk head and one names a group in the
drawer. **Whether they should be one string is yours.**

**Two strings the map needed and did not have to invent:**

**1. The Voice anchor became a station that retracts.** Ship B gave the voice
a header and no chevron on your explicit ruling: "it has a header and no
contents, and collapsing it would hide `Calibrate`, the only entry to the
ritual, for no height." The three-frame map answers that rather than
overriding it: **Calibrate is the station's contents now, not its header**, so
it is one press away rather than hidden, and every station in a frame
retracts. Shut, the row costs 41 px and says the voice is there. Open, it is
the same anchor line it always was. Reversing it is one `ontoggle` removed.

**2. Underlay left Corrections and is its own station again.** N.92 slice 4
dissolved the Shift Lyrics station and put the syllabified text inside
`CorrectionSurface`'s LYRIC row, on your walk of `2238e8b`. The three-group
map names Underlay as its own station in Score markup, and it is the later
ruling. **Only the queue and the cursor moved.** The lyric verbs stay in
Corrections with the note they act on, because they render only when a paired
note is selected, and their label and their placed-syllable counter stay with
them. Nothing is drawn twice.

**3. Corrections gained a station header and a chevron**, which it did not
have. `CorrectionSurface`'s own CORRECTIONS heading is gated to the dock in
the same ship (`CorrectionSurface.svelte:437`), so the word is drawn once. The
dock is untouched.

**4. The 2 px sage boundary is retired inside a group**, replaced by a 1 px
hairline. Your ruling of 2026-08-20 was that a drawer with one horizontal is
worth more than a drawer that grades its horizontals. Inside a frame it is a
STATION that ends, not a region; the band is what says a region ends, and it
says it louder than 2 px did. The takeover's rule is the one declaration of
that treatment still standing, and it is increment 3's.

**5. The binder row became a station.** N.65 ship B §B.6 ruled it a bare row
so it would relate to the score field the way Clear and Transcribe relate to
the text field. The map names "Export and import" as a station in Piece, and
the field it sat under is now headerless and never closed, so a bare row at
its foot would be the orphan control the GUI spec's §3.3 forbids.

**6. The pinned anchors are gone**, both of them, with their sage and lavender
rules. The lavender is not lost: the voice is inside the Score markup group
and that band is `--lang-chip-marked`, the same hue one step down.

**7. Learn and Guide keep `--drawer-bg` on the slab.** The ruling says the
slab takes the desk's surround. Their drawer holds a table of contents and no
groups, so there is no paper card to cut a divot out of and a surround there

**8. The notices sit last in the Score markup group.** They were at the foot
