The desktop lip: three shapes

Real tokens: drawer #FAF8F5, surround #6B6560. The lip's job: say "this edge moves" without shouting. Hue stays neutral (hue names place, ink names state); the chevron flips direction with the drawer's state. Hover deepens contrast; nothing animates but opacity and position.
