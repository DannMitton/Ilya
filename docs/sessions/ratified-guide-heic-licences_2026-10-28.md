## English

A photograph in Apple’s HEIC format is opened by libheif (© 2017–2020 struktur AG; © 2017–2025 Dirk Farin), with the libde265 HEVC decoder (© 2013–2014 struktur AG, Dirk Farin, and the openHEVC contributors), in Kiril Vatev’s libheif-js build; all are released under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3. HEVC, the compression inside a HEIC photograph, is covered by patents that their holders license through patent pools, among them Access Advance’s HEVC Advance pool; <em>Ilya</em> uses HEVC only to decode a photograph the singer chooses, and holds no licence from these pools.
