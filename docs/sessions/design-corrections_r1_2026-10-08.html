Corrections, redesigned (r1, 2026-10-08)

Twelve states, each drawn at the desk (1440 by 900) and on a phone (390 by 844). The Loupe card, the Paper behind it, and the floating song-tempo control are sketched only to place the panel; the panel is the design. Tokens are the app’s own (lavender for the melody, rose ink for removal, ink tertiary for hints). Every drawing is a mockup written by hand for this return, not a capture of the running app. Notes: design-corrections_r1_2026-10-08.md.

1. The panel at rest

Nothing in focus. The last station used stays chosen; the two lines under the rail say what to take.

2. A note in focus

Length is open (the forecast’s fourth most-needed control, and the most common single repair). The rail is the same for every focus.

3. A caret in focus

Stations that need a note go quiet; Length now enters a note, and Tempo, Dynamic, and Hairpin are live. The caret is taken as today: no new taps.

4. A flagged bar arrives (the reader-fault mark)

Tapping a flagged bar raises the Loupe on Corrections with Not voice open and “This bar” ready. Nothing changed; it asks.

5. Removing a stretch that is not the voice

From here to a bar the singer reaches with Next or the arrow keys; the count updates; one act removes it; Undo brings it back.

6. A triplet over a run

Three eighths under a bracket, in the space of two. The bar total fixes itself; Other… opens today’s “N of M in the space of”.

7. A double sharp, and its carry

The five accidentals in one row; “Carries through the bar” is on by default and says what it reaches.

8. Placing a tempo

At a caret: kind, number, Place. The song-wide tempo stays in its own floating control; the beat unit is set only there.

9. Placing a dynamic

One tap places it at the caret (undoable). The torso marks it as the singer’s own.

10. Placing a hairpin

Kind, then where it ends. The caret is the first endpoint; “to end of bar” or a second caret is the other.

11. A rit., default termini

Starts at the caret, runs to the next tempo mark, arrives at 75 percent. Dashed line to the arrival, as print.

12. A rit. ended at a second caret

The singer ends it at another caret. Arrival shows at the end, nudged with − and +.
