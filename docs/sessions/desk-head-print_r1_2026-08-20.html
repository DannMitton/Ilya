What the drawing rules.

  • Print sits immediately to the right of the pair, not centred in the span between the pair and the reading links, and not appended to the pair as a third segment.
  • A gap separates it from the pair. The gap is the whole point: the pair is a choice between two destinations, and Print is an action. Joining them would say they are the same kind of thing.
  • It is a box in the pair's own idiom: same height, same hairline border, same baseline, same uppercase letterspaced label.
  • It is desk furniture, not paper. It sits on the desk field above the sheet's top edge, and it disappears at print with the rest of the desk head.

It is always live. Dann ruled that a singer may print any of the four destinations, and that page parameters belong in the browser's own print dialog. So there is no disabled state, no greying, and no vanishing on Learn and Guide. The bar is identical on all four destinations by construction rather than by a condition.

What is NOT established. The exact gap, the fill, and whether Print carries the pair's border or sits bare on the desk. The drawing shows a bordered box and this rendering follows it. Measure the desk head at 360 x 640 with Print added, and report a collision rather than shrinking anything.

And one consequence Dann ruled the same minute. A printed Learn or Guide excerpt must carry a header and a footer identifying its source. ReadingPaper.svelte has neither today: its whole print block removes a box shadow. That is separate work and it is not numbered yet.
