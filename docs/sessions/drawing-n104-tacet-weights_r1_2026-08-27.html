N.104 — the tacet bar, at three weights each

Every plate is the real renderer's real output, on a synthetic four-quarter phrase: one sung bar, a run of tacet bars, one sung bar, justified to the page's own 672 px line at the production stave of 5.5 px. Each variant is shown at true size and again at four times. The bar's thickness and the proportion of its end-serifs are the font's, not this desk's: Finale Maestro carries restHBarLeft, restHBarMiddle and restHBarRight. The three numbers drawn here are the ones nothing in the project establishes.

A. How long the tacet bar is

The measure the consolidated rest occupies, barline to barline, in stave-spaces. Fixed for every run length, so a run of 2 and a run of 30 take the same room. TACET_REST.measureSp, drawn here at 8, 12 and 16.

At true size, the whole system

measureSp 8
measureSp 12
measureSp 16

At three and a half times, cropped to the mark

measureSp 8
measureSp 12
measureSp 16

B. How big the numeral is

A multiple of the notation glyph size, and the digits are the time-signature digits, so 1 is the size of a time signature. TACET_REST.numeralScale, drawn here at 0.8, 1 and 1.25.

C. How far the numeral clears the staff

Stave-spaces from the top staff line to the bottom of the numeral. TACET_REST.numeralClearanceSp, drawn here at 0.5, 0.9 and 1.5.

The shipped defaults are measureSp 12, barInsetSp 1.25, numeralScale 1 and numeralClearanceSp 0.9. They are recorded in packages/score-parser/src/staff-renderer.ts as convention rather than as Gould, because the priors memo says at its own line 220 that pp. 34 to 38 are unphotographed and no dimensional prior exists for a rest of any kind. If the source is photographed, these are the numbers to check.
