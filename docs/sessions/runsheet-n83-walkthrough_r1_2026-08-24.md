# Run sheet: the colleague walkthrough, r1

Serves N.83. Zoom with screen share; the colleague drives by remote control.
Fill the two bracketed slots before the call. Length: roughly 20 minutes,
an estimate, not a measurement.

## Before the call

- Open `[deploy URL]` in a clean Chrome window. English interface throughout.
- Have on the desktop: the 400 dpi PNG of the Lamm scan (Without Sun, song 1)
  and `[the engraved score file]`.
- Close every window you do not intend to share.

## The walk

1. **Arrival.** The colleague looks and does not click. Your line: "Ilya makes
   a study edition for singers of Russian song. The paper you see is the
   interface itself."
2. **Transcribe.** The colleague pastes a Russian poem, or loads the prepared
   song. IPA appears on the page. Invite: "Click any word." The Inspector
   opens in the drawer, the correction redraws the page. Your line: "The page
   is the control. Nothing Ilya prints carries a widget."
3. **Fit.** Load `[the engraved score]`. Show the voice profile roster and the
   watch band. Your line: "Fit forecasts whether a piece suits a voice. It
   never declares."
4. **Learn.** The colleague opens two of the seven sections. Optional: click
   « Français » here; this chrome is walked in both languages.
5. **Print.** Open the print preview of a transcription or score markup. Your
   line: "Print renders the paper. The page you studied is the page you hold."
6. **The reader, framed honestly.** The colleague uploads the PNG. It reads in
   about six seconds and renders: 3 systems, 9 staves, 57 notes. Your line:
   "The mechanism runs end to end on a 1931 scan. The notation it renders is
   not yet accurate. That is the open work, and it is measured, not guessed."
7. **The contrast.** Point back at the engraved page from beat 3. Your line:
   "From an engraved file, the same paper is accurate. The gap is scan
   recognition alone."

  rewritten."
- French mode on Fit. The watch band prints English there (N.82).
- Raw IMSLP PDFs. JBIG2 compression, unreadable today. Upload the PNG only.
