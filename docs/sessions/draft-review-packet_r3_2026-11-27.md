## THE PACKET

### What Ilya is

Ilya is a free, open-source tool for singers working in Russian. You give it a song, by file or by photographing the
printed page, and it reads the music and the words and gives you three documents about that song:

- **TEXT**, the words as the score sets them, with IPA under each syllable and a gloss of each word.
- **MELODY**, your score drawn again with the IPA and the Russian seated under the notes, and the places this piece
  asks something particular of your voice marked on it.
- **INSIGHTS**, what those places are, described in plain terms, with a cited strategy for each and the judgement
  left to you.

It runs in a browser, on a phone or a computer. Nothing is uploaded: the reading happens on your own machine, and
your scores and recordings stay there.

It is built by one singer and scholar, and it has never been outside the room it was made in. That is what this is
for.

### What I would like you to do

Open **ilya.dannmitton.com** and bring a song you already know in Russian, ideally one you have sung. A PDF or a
MusicXML file works, and so does a photograph of the printed page.

Spend as long or as little as you like. There is no script and nothing you can break. **If it helps, switch to
French at the top right**; the whole application is in both languages.
