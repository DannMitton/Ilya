Three things the board exposes

--stone-600 does not exist. It is written seven times as var(--stone-600, #57534e), and because it is never declared, every one of those seven falls through to the hex. The colour on screen is right; the token is a ghost.

Insights and Learn share a desk. --surround-insights and --surround-learn are both #DBCACA, both commented "dusty rose at 60%". Two names, one value. Your 2026-08-19 ruling was that every distinct working surface carries its own hue, and these two currently do not. Umber is the obvious candidate if you ever want them apart.

Four hues, but eight lavenders and sages. --light-sage and --light-lavender carry four uses each. They are not part of the three-value grammar; they predate it.
