## 3. Rank 1: B0 and B0g

**The singer.** On a system Ilya reads, a word of two or three syllables printed under a melisma now lands its second syllable where the print puts it, not a third of the way along the word's box. She sees nothing new; she sees fewer syllables one note early or late. A grace note before a long note no longer takes the syllable that belongs to the long note.

**What the readers give.** Each word's `symbols[]` with `text` and `bbox` (`index.d.ts:191-210`). `wordsOf` drops them (`underlay-recognize.ts:55-56`).

**Where it lives and what crosses the boundary.** `WordBox` (`underlay-page.ts:46-52`) gains an optional `symbols: { text, x0, x1 }[]` (page pixels after `unframeBox`, as the word box is at `:209-210`); `Token` (`underlay-seat.ts:76-83`) gains the same, optional, so `splitTokens` (`underlay-tokens.ts:63-69`) and `cutUnits` (`underlay-units.ts:79-85`) can place each piece by the box of the piece's own vowel letter, and fall back to the letter share where a piece's vowel has no symbol (the B0 report: "every token's symbols matched its letters, fallbacks 0", so on row 114 f1 the fallback never ran). `voiceNotes` (`underlay-prepare.ts:114-141`) gains three tests on the `<note>` body (`<grace`, `<chord`, and `<voice>` above 1) and leaves such a note out of `NotePlace`s, counted into `UnderlayStats`. No seat crosses into `score/`; the join is untouched. `UNDERLAY_READER` becomes `underlay@5` (`stamp.ts:38-55`: bumped when the reader's output changes, so a kept reading is read again once).

**The comments' substance.** On the vowel-box placement: what it checks (the piece's vowel letter has a symbol box), why (the vowel is the nucleus, Dann 2026-11-05 00:32; the note takes a vowel, 00:35; §0.2 positions from ink), what follows when it fails (the letter-share placement, counted as `fallbacks`, which the B0 measure found 0 times on row 114 f1), how it relates (the piece's x then feeds `seatTokens`' `monotone` unchanged).
