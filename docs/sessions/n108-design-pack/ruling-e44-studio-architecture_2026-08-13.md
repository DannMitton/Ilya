## §CONTRADICTIONS. Where two ruled things disagree

1. **Stations versus anchors (N.65).** E.27 §3.3: five accordion stations plus two takeovers. E.36 §1.4: metadata and NOTATION pinned top, voice pinned bottom, a scroll between. Both ruled. **They compose:** the anchors are the frame, and the stations are what the scroll between them holds.
2. **The Inspector's home.** E.27 made the Inspector a full-drawer takeover. E.36 §1.2 rules the inspector pane and the Analysis section are one, and the tree implements a resident region. I resolve against my own E.27 takeover.
3. **The Packet's continuous page versus the document selector.** E.27 §3.2 specified one continuous three-sheet document. Dann ruled the documents stay separate behind a selector. E.27 §3.2 is dead; nobody builds a sheet indicator.
4. **NOTATION's position.** E.36 §1.4 (top anchor) wins over E.29 (bottom).
5. **The accent-follows-tab ruling becomes unsatisfiable** under Studio; E.36 §1.4 implies the answer (NOTATION sage, voice anchor lavender).
6. When `INCLUDE_SHANE` is false the pair is not rendered at all and Studio shows one document.
7. The 229 px pair measurement is invalidated; the labels are unruled.

## §PERSPECTIVE

**Tonight's ruling is correct, and it corrects me.** I overturn my own E.27 §3.2, the continuous three-sheet packet page. A selector that chooses a document is the honest form. What survives of E.27: one destination, one intake, three rooms.

**The drawer contradiction (N.65) dissolves.** Composed: the top anchor holds Piece and NOTATION, the bottom anchor holds the voice, and the scroll between holds Source, Analysis, and Output.

**On the Inspector I rule against my own takeover.** The Analysis region always has a truthful tenant. The takeover survives for exactly one thing, the calibration ritual, which is phased, mic-gated, and genuinely modal. **One takeover, not two.**

**On colour as the wayfinding answer.** Colour alone is not sufficient, by the project's own measurement: the four tokens are near-greys, chroma 0.045 to 0.073, and the desks sit close in value. But colour is not alone: the desk hue, the active card's ink border, the reading labels' underline, and the document's own face. Sufficient, provided the pair's active state never depends on hue alone.

**On timing, said plainly.** None of this blocks the ratified line. Ratify the direction, record the supersessions, and **not build any of it before the beta line closes**, unless Dann displaces a named blocker.
