Formant profile: a map of your voice’s resonances

Nothing is claimed about your voice

Ilya has read your score, but no voice has been measured, so there is nothing to compare this line against.

Every acoustic mark: no crossings, no timbre turns.
The watch list, entirely. An empty list is the truthful output here.
Any reading of your range, your tessitura, or your passaggio.

The stave carries no marks because none can be earned.
