## The rules

1. **One source of keys on Insights.** The new search runs whether or not the written key is in range, and replaces both of today's sources (`searchBetterKey`, `singer-page.ts:174-205`, and the range finding's `transposition`, `InsightsPane.svelte:302-322`), and the unlikely branch's single key (`singer-blocks.ts:174-186`). Markup's ruler is untouched.
2. **Reach:** six semitones either way (`KEY_SEARCH_WINDOW`, unchanged; Dann 13:55).
3. **Two challenges, pitch-dependent only** (Dann 13:47 to 13:50):
   - **Range:** inside; beyond by up to two semitones, above or below (a named challenge); beyond that, not workable by default (shown only under the reveal). Its own constant, `RANGE_CHALLENGE_MARGIN = 2`, agreed by Dann 13:51, not a reuse of `UNLIKELY_TESSITURA_MARGIN`. A compass wider than the range at both ends beyond the margin is not workable.
   - **Passaggio time:** the share of sung time at or above the primo (`shareAtOrAbovePrimo`, `singer-page.ts:49`) and the share above the secondo (`shareAboveSecondo`, `:55`), with the zones as `insights.ts:503-507` defines them, unchanged; sung time as `aggregatePhonation` sums it (`insights.ts:522-526`). The longest held note above the secondo is the example a line may name, not a separate challenge.
   - **Tessitura is neither a challenge nor a gate** (today's gate at `singer-page.ts:192` goes). **Crossings are not a challenge** (the gate at `:194` goes).
4. **Grouping by challenge set** (Fable's codification of Dann's "the number of keys follows the data", adopted by the desk): a key's set is discrete: range (inside; up to two semitones beyond, above or below), any time at or above the primo (yes or no), any time above the secondo (yes or no), a held note above the secondo (yes or no). Keys with the same set form one group. Within a group the smallest move is shown (Dann 13:38), but only a move that changes something that matters: a challenge gained or lost, or a share moved by at least 10 points against the written key (`BETTER_KEY_MIN_DROP`, `singer-page.ts:41`, JUDGEMENT). A group with no such key is not shown, nor is a group whose set equals the written key's unless a share moved by 10 points. Shares go on the line as figures; they never decide the group.
5. **Order: by stakes for this singer, then smallest move** (RULED by Dann 14:09, option 1 of three, the desk's recommendation, on N.173's rubric: a moment's stakes are the size of its demands for this singer, adjusted by their intake answer for that demand, `draft-curation-rules_r1_2026-09-24.md` r3). A group's stakes = the sum over its challenges of a weight from the singer's answer to that challenge's question (rule 8): `intakeBand` points 1 to 2 weigh 1.0, point 3 weighs 0.75, points 4 to 5 weigh 0.5, and "not sure" or no answer weighs 1.0 (DESK DEFAULT, JUDGEMENT, one table of constants). Lower stakes first; then the smaller share at or above the primo; then the smallest move; then the lower key. This is the comments' fallback (`apps/web/src/lib/insights/comments.ts:426-433`, `byStakes`) with the intake weighting added. **At most three groups** (DESK DEFAULT, Dann's to strike); the rest sit under the reveal. Report how often the cap binds.
6. **Vowels past their turn are described, never counted as a challenge, and never framed as unusual for a voice in which they are commonplace.** Dann 13:49 to 13:50: *"Let's not pathologize what's normal for a large cohort of singers."* A line mentions them only where keys differ by the 10-point test on the share past the turn. **Only vowels whose fR1 is `'sung'` or `'derived'` count** (`fR1Source`, `packages/score-parser/src/analysis-types.ts:69`); a `'surrogate'` (placeholder) vowel is left out, and if no vowel is left the line is omitted. Report how many fixture vowels are surrogate.
7. **Data first, label second** (Dann 13:53): the typed range and passaggi decide; nothing here reads the declared voice type. The contrast line between label and data is QUEUE row 9's, not this row's.
8. **Wording by the singer's answers, same keys for everyone** (Dann 13:53: the stage changes only the wording; 14:04: *"Ilya already effectively asks each singer their experience stage, from novice to expert through those seven questions. The radio button choices are finely graded and we can use those responses"*). The intake is `IntakeAnswers` (`packages/score-parser/src/analysis-types.ts:113-137`), five points a question, banded by `intakeBand` (`apps/web/src/lib/insights/comments.ts:98-101`). Each challenge's line follows its own question's band: a range challenge above, Q1 (`top`); below, Q2 (`bottom`); passaggio time, Q3 (`passaggi`); a held note above the secondo, Q4 (`sustained`). Points 1 to 2: add a clause of why; point 3: as drafted; points 4 to 5: terse. The register of every line follows Q7 (`registerFor`, `comments.ts:106-109`). Answers never change which keys are shown.
9. **Missing data:** without typed passaggi the set has the range axis only; without a typed range there is no search and no line.
