The spine: a sung note is two choices meeting, a pitch and a vowel. Pitch is many frequencies heard as one note; a vowel is a set of resonances heard as colour. Where a harmonic of the pitch crosses a resonance of the vowel, the colour turns. Both are knowable from the score and your voice, so the turn can be foreseen. MELODY draws that forecast.

Worked example, carried throughout: Dann's [ɑ] (fR1 617 Hz, pitch of turning 308.5 Hz, D♯4 −15 cents) against his [i] (fR1 296 Hz, turning 148 Hz, D3 +14 cents): the same voice turns over an octave apart on two vowels.
