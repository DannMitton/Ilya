## 2. What was built

This is memo §6.2, items 1 to 4, with §6.4 governing. Nothing is stored.

1. **`drawPairings`** (`pairings.ts:979`).
   - It builds a drawn copy of the raw `lines`.
   - Each word is re-cut by `reslicedSyllables` (`syllable-utils.ts:252`), which is now the one
     rule for both pages. `applyOpenSyllabificationToLines` calls it too, so its behaviour is
     unchanged.
   - The word is then reconstituted where the global switch, inverted by a spot key, says so
     (`reconstitutedWord`, `pairings.ts:930`). That is one positional walk over the whole word,
     which is what Transcription does, split back into syllables.
   - It runs the existing `buildSlotQueue` over the copy and answers each seat by
     `(line, word, slot)` plus `origin.word`.
   - A seat that fails the lookup draws its stored text.
2. **`drawnUnderlay`** (`+page.svelte:2330`). It sits below the switch state it reads, not beside
   `shownPairings` at `:420`. That is a placement choice, not a design change. It is passed to
   `InsightsPane` (`:4866`) and `VoiceProfilePane` (`:4883`).
3. **`VoiceProfilePane.svelte`.**
   - The drawn IPA feeds `ipaPreview` (`:628`), and the four spelling switches still apply after
     it.
   - The drawn vowel feeds the forecast (`:597`), through a new optional third parameter on
     `withPairedVowel` (`pairings.ts:766`).
   - The drawn acute feeds `cyrPreview` (`:668-669`).
   - The loupe takes both previews unchanged.
4. **`InsightsPane.svelte:112`** takes the drawn vowel.

**The acute's four conditions now live once.** They are `wordTakesAcute` (`pairings.ts:863`) and
`withAcute` (`:871`), and `stressAcutedCyrillic` and the drawing step both call them.
