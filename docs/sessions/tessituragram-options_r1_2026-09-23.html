The tessituragram with phonation time: three layouts

Illustrative numbers only. Not your voice, not a real song. Passaggi drawn at A3 and D4. Bold [i] stands for a vowel that carries a finding.

A. Titze and Maxfield's form, vowels beside

Pitch runs left to right, seconds run up. Zone shares ride on the shading. The vowel list sits to the right.

B. One picture: each bar split by vowel

Same axes as A, but each pitch's bar is divided by the vowels sung on it. You see where [i] sits against your passaggi without reading across.

C. Pitch runs up, as on a stave

High notes at the top, bars run right. The passaggi are horizontal lines, the way a singer pictures them.
