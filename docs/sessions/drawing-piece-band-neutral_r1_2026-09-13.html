The quietest and the darkest. Reads almost as a title bar over the two working bands, which may be exactly right or may be too much hierarchy.

What to look for. Not whether the grey is handsome on its own, but whether the row still reads as one drawer. Cobalt makes three peers of three different kinds. A neutral makes one label and two working surfaces, which is the claim that metadata is apparatus rather than the singer's work.

One measured thing, not a reason to choose. The coloured bands carry white at 3.0 to 3.7, below 4.5 for small text; that is already true today of sage and lavender and is not created by anything here. The neutrals at 4.80 and 5.77 happen to be the only bands in the drawer that would pass.
