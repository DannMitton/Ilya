# The Gould shot list. One page, built from the record, 2026-09-14

**Why this exists.** `STATE.md` has carried a "Gould re-shoot, four spots" item
since 2026-08-29. Tonight the list grew twice: N.138 SPENT a flagged figure, and
N.138 increment 3 raised a question no extraction answers. Only Dann can close
any of it, because the book is not on this machine.

**Nothing here is urgent and nothing here blocks a build.** It exists so that if
the book is ever open in front of him, the whole list closes in one pass instead
of one flag at a time across five sessions.

**How to shoot, from what the flags themselves say.** Every "low-confidence
numeral" flag blames small diagram or table numerals in the source photograph. So
shoot each spot CLOSE and SQUARE, one page per frame, with the numerals legible
rather than the whole spread in shot. A second frame of the same page at a
different angle costs nothing and has settled a contradiction before.

## Tier 6. A question with no page yet. NEW 2026-09-14

**How an engraved excerpt TERMINATES.** Dann ruled on 2026-09-14 that the loupe's
excerpt shows its stave running a little past the closing barline, and that only
the bar ending the piece stops flush. **No extracted rule covers it.** The four
barline priors on disk, r96 p. 39, r224 p. 240, r98 p. 43 and r242 p. 42, are all
about weight, spacing and clearance.

**The way in is the index**, not a page number: look up "excerpt", "incipit", and
"barline, final" and shoot whatever they point at. The one-stave-space protrusion
Ilya draws today is the desk's figure and is not Gould's, and the record says so.

## What NOT to bother with

Gould explicitly leaves these unquantified, so a photograph cannot settle them and
nobody should spend a frame on them: the numeric value of "steep" for a beam angle
(r260), "no obvious case" for the beamed-group down-stem default (r280), and the
threshold between a "short" rest and a "longer" one for whether an extender breaks
(r36).
