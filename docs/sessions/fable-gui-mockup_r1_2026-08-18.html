Arrival, then reading. The fitted page greets and orients; one tap enters the readable scroll; one tap returns. Honest and legible, at the cost of a fourth level of disclosure and one more concept for the singer to hold.

Principles applied in every exhibit (from the ratified slate):

· Hue names place, ink names state: lavender appears in Studio only on the voice line and its takeover; selection is carried by the ink border of the boxed pair.

· No orphan controls: the calibration plea is gone from the scroll; every control sits in a labelled station.

· Station order invariant across documents; anchors never scroll away.

· Three radii only: square paper, small-radius controls, full-round toggle and pill.

· One duration: every transition here is 180 ms ease-out, opacity and transform only; the paper itself never animates, it is replaced.

Instrument and stand-ins. Sage #8B9A7D, the two papers, and the lavender desk hex are documented values; the lavender, rose, and cobalt accents and both desk tints are stand-ins read by eye from tonight's walk of deploy ilya-b0gn6a3ru and must be re-verified against app.css before any build. Typefaces are stand-ins (Sorts Mill Goudy / Palatino stack for the paper; system sans for the instrument); the tree's font-family declarations were not read this session. IPA is as walked, approximate. The Studio bench draws the E.44 Fable ruling (§PERSPECTIVE and §PLAN S1 to S4) and decides nothing that ruling did not; the portrait bench decides nothing at all, it is the exhibit for a ruling Dann has not yet made. English only; French strings are not designed here. Mockup by Fable, 2026-08-18.
