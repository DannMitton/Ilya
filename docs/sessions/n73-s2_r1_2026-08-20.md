## 1. What shipped

**The drawer stops swapping.** `Drawer.svelte:156` renders `rootPanel` and
`shanePanel` both, always, in that order, on both of Studio's documents. The
old `{:else if activeTab === 'shane'}` branch is gone. Learn and Guide are
untouched, and the NOTATION anchor's condition at `Drawer.svelte:423` is
unchanged.

**The second `MetadataFields` is deleted.** The survivor is
`RootPanel.svelte:184`, and it now carries `fromScore` and `onrevert`, threaded
from `+page.svelte:1828-1829` through two new props. The
`{#if arrangerProvenance}` block moved with it to `RootPanel.svelte:190-193`,
directly beneath the metadata block, and its `.shane-provenance` rule travelled
to `RootPanel.svelte:602`. Svelte scopes a rule to the component that authors
the markup, so leaving the rule in `+page.svelte` would have dropped the line's
style silently and raised an unused-selector warning at the same time.

**The second Print button is deleted**, with its `.shane-button-row` wrapper and
the `.shane-button-row`, `.shane-print-btn`, and `.shane-print-btn:disabled`
rules. The survivor is `RootPanel.svelte:277`, and its guard is keyed on the
visible document by `printDisabled` (`+page.svelte:450-454`):
`!ingestedScore && Object.keys(shaneFormants).length === 0` on the marked
score, `!hasResults` on the transcription. Both expressions are the ones the
two buttons carried, verbatim. Nothing is invented and neither behaviour is
lost.

**The twinned song rows are deleted.** `.shane-binder-row` and its three
buttons are gone, with the `.shane-binder-row` and `.shane-binder-btn` rules.
`RootPanel`'s binder row and song list survive, one of each. `.binder-input`
stays: `RootPanel`'s Import still clicks it.

**Score intake joined the Source region.** `ScoreUploader`, its `{#key doc.id}`,
and the `{#if noLyricsFile}` notice now render inside `RootPanel`, directly
under the textarea and the wired scanner, through a `sourceScore` snippet
(`+page.svelte:1843`, rendered at `RootPanel.svelte:252`). The
`{#if INCLUDE_SHANE}` gate came with them and still wraps the score half.

I used a snippet rather than props here, and the brief did not say which. The
uploader's wiring is its restore source, its key on the open song, its arrival
handler, and the feature gate. Threading four more props into `RootPanel` to
move markup would have pushed the panel toward the twenty-prop shape §2 rejects,
and `consoleContent` is the same pattern already: `+page.svelte` renders
`InspectorPanel` inside `RootPanel`'s console region. The markup sits inside
`RootPanel` either way.

**`lib/destinations.ts:13-19`** now says the stored-tab migration is S3, cites
this brief's §6, and records why the coordinating desk moved it.

**One line changed outside the drawer**, `ProfileSwitcher.svelte:196`. §2
carries it.

## 2. Where the tree beat this brief

**Only one pointer was stale, and it was not in the brief.** Every line number
the brief gave was correct at `81438d4`. Two comments in the tree were not.

**`+page.svelte`'s claim that Transcription's Print button has no disabled
style is false.** The deleted `.shane-print-btn` comment said "RECORDED, NOT
CHASED: `RootPanel.svelte:213-219` has no `:disabled` rule at all, so
Transcription's Print button looks enabled while disabled."
`RootPanel.svelte` has carried `.action-btn:disabled { opacity: 0.45;
cursor: not-allowed; }` the whole time, and the Print button is an
`.action-btn`. The one surviving button greys out on both documents. Nothing
had to be added, and the note died with the rule that carried it.

**`.start-over` says it twins `.shane-print-btn`**, which this step deleted. Its
own declarations are unchanged and match `RootPanel`'s `.action-btn`; I
rewrote the comment to say so.

**`MetadataFields.svelte`'s header said the Transcription drawer never passes
`fromScore`.** It does now. Both comments there are updated, and the prop stays
optional.

### The one thing this step broke, and the one line that fixes it

**`ProfileSwitcher.svelte` focuses its profile-name field on mount on a fine
pointer**, so the merged drawer opened scrolled to its own bottom: a singer
opening the drawer met the calibration wizard instead of the metadata block.
That field used to sit near the top of the Fit drawer's own short column. Under
one drawer it sits at the foot of a column roughly twice as tall, and the
browser scrolls a focused element into view.

MEASURED on 2026-08-20, `1400x900`, fresh tab, drawer open:

| `shanePanel` | drawer `scrollTop` on load | `scrollHeight` |
|---|---|---|
| rendered | 1160.5 | 1161 |
| temporarily not rendered | 0 | 1704 |

The fix is `node.focus({ preventScroll: true })` at
`ProfileSwitcher.svelte:196`. The focus and the once-only selection are
untouched, so typing still replaces the default name; only the scroll side
effect is gone. **This is JUDGEMENT, not a ruling of yours, and it is one word
to reverse.** I shipped it because the alternative was a drawer that opens on
the wrong end of itself, and because `preventScroll` removes a side effect that
the function's own comments never claimed to want.

### Where the merged order does not match §4

§4 asks for Analysis (7), Voice (8), then Output (9). Rendering `rootPanel`
then `shanePanel` cannot produce that, because Output lives in `RootPanel` and
Voice lives in `shanePanel`. The shipped order is:

1. Dictionary status
2. Song Setup: the one `MetadataFields`, then arranger provenance
3. Source: textarea and OCR overlay, then `ScoreUploader`, then `noLyricsFile`
4. Transcribe, Print, and Clear, in the one `.button-row`
5. Output: export and import, then the song rows
6. Analysis: the Inspector region
7. Score work: `SyllableStation`, then `ShiftLyricsControl`
8. Notices: `binderError`, `binderNotice`, `orphanedCount`, `slotQueue`,
   `doc.remoteChange`
9. Voice: `CalibrationWizard`

Items 1 through 3 and 7 through 9 are §4's order exactly. Reaching §4's 4, 7,
and 9 needs two moves the brief forbids elsewhere: splitting Print out of the
Clear-Print-Transcribe grid, which is the grid `ENVIRONMENT.md` §The two Print
buttons keeps a control's x-position in, and moving `RootPanel`'s Output block
past `CalibrationWizard`, which contradicts §3.2 and §3.3 keeping the survivors
where they are. §4 says it is provisional and S3 rules the stations, so I left
the order to S3 and named it here rather than inventing a station ruling.

### Two cosmetic consequences, both named rather than chased

**`.shane-panel` lost its 20 px top padding** (`+page.svelte:2186`, now
`padding: 0 1rem 40px`). The two panels' paddings met in the middle of one
column and read as a seam. `.root-panel`'s 40 px bottom now carries that gap
alone. JUDGEMENT, one line to reverse.

**The lavender focus ring narrowed.** `.shane-panel :global(:focus-visible)`
(`+page.svelte:2191`) gave Fit's surfaces a `--deeper-lavender` outline. The
metadata fields and the score uploader left that selector's scope when they
moved into `RootPanel`, so they take the global sage ring now. Under one drawer
one focus colour is arguably the right answer, but that is a taste ruling and
it is yours, not mine. I changed nothing to cause it and nothing to correct it.

## 5. What you must walk

On the phone if you can, because a desktop cannot falsify a mobile print bug.

1. Open the drawer on **Transcription**, then tap **Marked score**. Nothing in
   the drawer appears, disappears, or moves. The page and the desk change; the
   drawer does not. **This is the step. If it fails, the step did not ship.**
2. Open the drawer. It shows **METADATA** at the top, not the calibration
   wizard.
3. Edit the title once. It shows on both documents.
4. Count the Print buttons. There is one. Before you transcribe and before a
   score arrives, it is greyed on both documents.
5. Paste Russian text and press **Transcribe**. Print is now live on the
   transcription. Tap **Marked score**: Print goes grey again, because no score
   is in yet. Press Print from inside the drawer on the transcription and check
   that no drawer, header, or ribbon reaches the paper.
6. Drop a score. Print is live on the marked score too.
7. Count the song lists and the export and import row. There is one of each.
8. With a score whose header carries a title, type over the title, then press
   **Revert to score header**. The header's title comes back.
9. On a browser that had the marked score open before this ship, reload. It
   still lands on the marked score.

Items 1, 3, 8, and 9 I walked myself at `1400x900` and they hold. Item 2 holds
after the `preventScroll` fix. Item 4 holds. Item 6 holds. Item 7 holds. Item 5
is the one I could not reach.
