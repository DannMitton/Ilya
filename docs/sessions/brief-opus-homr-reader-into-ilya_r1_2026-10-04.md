## The need

Ilya is a free web app for classical singers (SvelteKit, runs entirely in the browser). A singer should be able to **drop a scan of a printed song (a PDF or a picture), see the melody, paste the poem, and see IPA and Russian seated under the notes.** Ilya already does everything after the notes. What it lacks is a reader that gets the notes right from a scan. Its own hand-built reader gets 455 of 805 notes right on five songs; homr gets 785. homr has a browser port, **homr-web**, and at 22:30 tonight it read a scanned page in 10.9 seconds on the owner's iMac (WebGPU), with output equal to desktop homr 0.7.0. **Your job: make Ilya use homr-web when a singer drops a scan.**
