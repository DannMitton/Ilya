**The discussion, kept for the record:**

- **Row 9.** He asked whether « Effacer » is idiomatic for Clear. The desk's
  answer: yes for a text field (Ilya already uses it, `notePicker.clear`), but a
  receipt removes a file, where « Retirer » reads more naturally. Put back to him.
- **Row 11.** He asked why the hint is so specifically nonspecific, and proposed
  *"Drop your file here."* The desk's reading: fair, because a dropped file is
  sorted by its kind, and a replacement already raises its own question. Draft:
  en "Drop your file here." / fr « Déposez votre fichier ici. »
- **Rows 12, 17, 18.** He asked why Ilya asks whether a PDF or a picture is the
  poem or the score, believing Ilya detects both. **It does not:**
  `ScoreUploader.svelte`, `take()`, sends every PDF and picture to the question
  (his ruling of 2026-09-03, when the camera icon was removed). Row 17's capital
  « The score » names the button in row 15. A design question is put to him.
