### 2. Corrupt record — WALKED, with two refutations

Stated before measuring: after setting `poem` to a number on one record through
IndexedDB and reloading, the row shows `song.unreadable`, opening it does not
crash, nothing overwrites it, and Export all carries it.

Set up through the app: two real songs typed in, then `poem` on the Pushkin song
edited to `42` in IndexedDB, which is what DevTools does by hand.

- Row marked: `"This song could not be read. It has been left untouched. You can
  still export it."`, under the song's OWN name and date.
- Opened it, typed `AN EDIT THAT MUST NEVER LAND`, waited past the debounce.
  Stored record unchanged: `poem: 42`, `updatedAt` still the corruption write's.
  Zero console errors.
- Fit drawer showed `song.unreadable`.

**REFUTATION 1, and the repair.** Export all wrote the REBUILT record for the
damaged song, plus the edit that was never saved. Cause: `exportBinder` took the
open song from the document and never asked the vault, and the damaged song was
the open one. So the salvage path failed for exactly the song the singer is
looking at. Repaired at `exchange.ts:100-108`: every song is read from the vault,
and the document wins only where the vault's answer carries no `raw`. Re-measured
on the rebuilt bundle: the binder's `song.json` holds `poem: 42` and the song's
own `updatedAt`. Regression tests: `salvage.test.ts`, "carries the raw record
even when the damaged song is the OPEN one" and "still takes the open song from
the DOCUMENT when it reads whole".

**Import into a clean origin.** Carried the binder to `127.0.0.1` (a separate
origin with separate storage), imported through the file input. Notice: "2 songs
were added." The damaged record landed with `poem: 42` (a number), its own
`updatedAt` unstamped, and its row still marked unreadable. **The round trip
preserves the damage rather than laundering it, which is the point.**

**REFUTATION 2, found while walking item 5 and repaired.** See item 5.

### 3. Newer schema — WALKED

Stated before measuring: raising a record's `schema` to 2 shows `song.newerIlya`

## 4. What I could not establish

- **Safari, on everything.** Nothing here ran on Safari. `persisted()`,
  `estimate()`, `QuotaExceededError`'s name on a WebKit IndexedDB transaction,
  and the anchor download are all places where Safari has its own answer.
- **Your deploy.** Every observation is from a local production build, not from
  a Vercel origin. Whether Chrome auto-grants persistence on YOUR deploy origin
  is not established; it did not on `127.0.0.1`, which is why item 1 walked.
- **A real private-browsing profile.** Item 5 is a stub, not a browser that
  genuinely refuses IndexedDB. What a real one does to `openLibraryDatabase`'s
  three-second timeout, rather than to a synchronous throw, is untested.
- **Whether `storage.saveFailed.quota` should be deleted.** It is now
  unreferenced: `storage.quotaFull` replaced it at the render site. I left it in
  place with a comment, because removing a ratified line is your call.
- **The empty-vault partial-loss case.** By construction Ilya cannot tell a
  wiped origin from a first visit, so a singer whose whole library was evicted
  gets no notice about it. That is design §4's own position, not a gap I closed.
- **The eviction notice on an origin that later becomes persistent.** The flag is
  one-way. A singer who installs Ilya to the home screen after seeing the notice
  is never told the risk has passed. Not a defect; not established as desirable
  either.
- **Whether the damaged record's SOURCE BYTES survive a corrupt-record round
  trip in a real browser.** They survive in the vitest round trip
  (`salvage.test.ts`), but the walk's damaged song had no score file attached,
  so the byte half of item 2 is gate-checked and not walked.
- **W2, N.74, F7, F8.** Untouched, per the brief's §4. W2 (the blank paper after
  the honest reload) did not make any step above ambiguous.

**The named weakness, confirmed and not solved.** The storage notices render in
the **Fit** drawer only and the song list renders in the **Transcription** drawer
only. They are different drawers, so the unreadable mark and the unreadable
sentence are never on screen together. This bit twice during the walk: I read an
empty notice list on the wrong tab, and an empty row list on the other. It did
not make a step ambiguous, because I could switch tabs; a singer who never opens
Fit is never told their storage is full.
