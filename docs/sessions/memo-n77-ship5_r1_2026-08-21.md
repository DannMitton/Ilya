### THREE THINGS THE BRIEF DID NOT HAVE, AND ALL THREE CHANGE THE COUNT

**1. `LearnContent.svelte` carries 19 `&#160;?`, which the brief does not list.**
It counted 3 plain spaces in that file and stopped. The 19 are the same defect
in a different spelling, so the brief's invariant, "no space of any kind
precedes a `?`", could not have been met by removing only the 3.

**2. One `?` sits on its own source line.** `LearnContent.svelte:1562-1563` read
`…peuvent être palatalisées` then a newline, indentation, and `?</p>`. HTML
collapses that to one space, so it rendered as a spaced question mark while
matching no same-line search. **It was found in the built bundle, not in the
source**, and only after the first pass had already run. The scan was then
redone across line breaks.

**3. `Drawer.svelte` is a fourth file, and it is not on the brief's list.** Its
table of contents carries French labels that duplicate Guide headings:
`Que fait Ilya ?`, `Pourquoi une seule source ?`, and `Où va Ilya ?`. Two of
those mirror headings this ship changed, so leaving them would have put a space
in the drawer and none on the page, for the same sentence, in the same view.
**Fixed, and flagged here because it is outside the briefed scope.** Reverting
is three characters at `Drawer.svelte:526`, `:528`, and `:532`.

### The verification is the bundle, not the source

space or NBSP and then a `?`. **Before the Drawer fix: 3. After: 0.**
