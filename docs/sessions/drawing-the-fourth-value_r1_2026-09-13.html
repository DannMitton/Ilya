The trade, stated plainly, because it cuts against something the desk told you an hour ago. The page has been neutral on purpose: the hue sits around the paper and the paper takes no side. This answer puts the hue onto the paper, in one small place. What it buys is a printed page that says which of the three documents it is without being read.
