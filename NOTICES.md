# Notices

## Scholarly Authority

Ilya's phonological engine implements the rules described in:

> Grayson, Craig. "Russian Lyric Diction." DMA diss., The Ohio State University, 2012.

Craig Grayson's dissertation is the sole phonological authority for this software.
All transcription rules, vowel reduction logic, palatalization processes, and
assimilation behaviour are derived from his work.
